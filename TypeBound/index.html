QUICK GUIDE

How to race a friend

  1. Click Create room, then click Copy invite.
  2. Send the invite link to your friend.
  3. Your friend opens the invite and clicks Join room.
  4. Wait until both participant cards say Ready.
  5. Press Start race to begin together.

The room is temporary and expires after 10 minutes. Race data travels directly between both browsers.
