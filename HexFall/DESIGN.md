# Hexfall design direction

Hexfall is a torchlit tactical ledger: a tabletop strategy game presented as an artifact from a damaged archive.

- **ENERGY:** 2 / balanced. The interface should feel alert and consequential, not loud at every moment.
- **RHYTHM:** 2 / structured with breaks. Use a large battlefield or run-status focal point, then quieter lists and controls.
- **MOTION:** 2 / purposeful. Motion explains selection, danger, impact, and reward. Decorative motion yields to player-controlled intensity settings.
- **Palette:** charcoal stone and smoke form the ground; aged gold marks permanent knowledge; ember marks immediate danger or action; ward-cyan marks support, selection, and safe routes.
- **Typography:** a display serif gives the archive its carved, story-bearing voice; a compact mono face handles combat telemetry and labels because those values are read as instruments.
- **Layout:** the expedition hub leads with the next floor and formation, then offers permanent upgrades and past-run records. The Meta Tree owns the full progression catalog.
- **Texture and depth:** restrained tabletop shadows, carved borders, and bespoke SVG sigils connect the UI to the dungeon. Strong glow is reserved for a current decision, a permanent purchase, or a combat impact.
- **Copy:** name the action and its consequence. Keep fantasy nouns where they carry world identity, but do not let ornamental labels replace instructions.
