Privacy Policy

How PsReader handles data on this site and the demo projects it links to. Short version: no cookies, no ads, no analytics, and almost every project keeps your data on your own device.

Effective September 30, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy applies to psreader.site.je (including index.html, portfolio.html, privacy.html, and terms.html) and to every demo project the site links to: GestureLab, LOTM, Pudding Paradise, Scratchpad, TarotDraw, TarotMemory, MysticCoin, TypeBound, and Pomodoro Pet. Demo projects hosted on other domains have their own policies; this one covers the versions linked from this site.

Data stored in your browser

All projects except TypeBound store their data in the browser's local storage on your device. Nothing from those projects is sent to a server, and clearing the site's data removes it. What each project stores:

  • Root pages: theme and accent color preference.
  • LOTM: no stored data.
  • Pudding Paradise: customer reviews you submit to the demo.
  • Scratchpad: notes you write and the theme you pick.
  • TarotDraw: settings, sound preference, and saved readings with any notes.
  • TarotMemory: theme, difficulty, stars, and completed-pack history.
  • MysticCoin: a history of past flips.
  • Pomodoro Pet: focus timer state and pet progress.
  • TypeBound (local): selected mode, theme, sound, volumes, run history, leaderboard, streak, typing analytics, avatar image, and custom sounds.

Webcam use in GestureLab

GestureLab uses your camera to track hand movements. Video frames are processed in your browser with MediaPipe, then discarded. No frame is uploaded, recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. The model and code are loaded from CDNs at startup.

TypeBound cloud account

TypeBound is the one project with an optional cloud account. It runs on Supabase. If you sign in, Supabase stores your email address and display name. Your race scores and statistics are posted to a public leaderboard under that name. You can export or delete your local records from the app, and you can ask for your cloud account to be removed through the contact address below.

Multiplayer rooms

TypeBound racing rooms connect two browsers directly over WebRTC. Standard STUN servers and a TURN relay are used to establish the connection; race text and live stats go to your opponent and no one else. This is peer-to-peer and is not stored by this site.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts and code from Google Fonts, Fontshare, unpkg, cdnjs, and jsDelivr. Those providers receive standard request data such as your IP address when they serve their resources. TypeBound connects to Supabase for cloud accounts. Their privacy policies govern that handling.

No cookies, ads, or analytics

This site sets no cookies, shows no advertising, and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The typing analytics inside TypeBound are computed locally and never leave your device unless you post a race score to the leaderboard.

Your choices

  • Clear any project's data through your browser's site-data controls.
  • Export and import TypeBound records from the app's backup panel.
  • Ask for your TypeBound cloud account to be deleted.
