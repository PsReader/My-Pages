Privacy Policy

How PsReader handles data on the portfolio and linked demos. PsReader does not operate ads or first-party analytics. Some preferences or demo progress may be stored in your browser; TypeBound offers an optional cloud account. Third-party services, including Spotify, may receive data when loaded.

Effective October 8, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy applies to psreader.site.je (including index.html, portfolio.html, privacy.html, and terms.html) and the 13 demos linked from the site: Lord of the Mysteries Fan Wiki (LOTM), Tarot Draw, Tarot Memory, Mystic Coin, Pudding Paradise, GestureLab, Typebound, Scratchpad, Lumen, Pixelpress, Fandom Fate, Hexfall, and Cipherly. Some demos are hosted on separate domains and may have project-specific practices; check any privacy notice shown within a demo for its current details.

Data stored in your browser

Storage varies by project. The homepage and portfolio page store theme and accent-color preferences in your browser's local storage; the privacy and terms pages store the selected theme preference. Some demo apps may save settings, history, or progress in browser storage, while other tools process selected files locally. Practices can differ by demo and may change, so check any notice shown within an app for its current details. TypeBound's optional cloud account is described below.

Webcam use in GestureLab

GestureLab uses your camera to track hand movements. Video frames are processed in your browser with MediaPipe, then discarded. No frame is uploaded, recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. The model and code are loaded from CDNs at startup.

TypeBound cloud account

TypeBound is the one project with an optional cloud account. It runs on Supabase. If you sign in, Supabase stores your email address and display name. Your race scores and statistics are posted to a public leaderboard under that name. You can export or delete your local records from the app, and you can ask for your cloud account to be removed through the contact address below.

Multiplayer rooms

TypeBound racing rooms connect two browsers directly over WebRTC. Standard STUN servers and a TURN relay are used to establish the connection; race text and live stats go to your opponent and no one else. This is peer-to-peer and is not stored by this site.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts and code from Google Fonts, Fontshare, unpkg, cdnjs, and jsDelivr. Those providers receive standard request data such as your IP address when they serve their resources. The homepage and portfolio also embed a Spotify playlist player. When the player loads, your browser connects directly to Spotify. Spotify's privacy policy describes technical data it may collect through its services, including URL information, cookie data, IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, network details, language, and operating system. Spotify's use of data and cookies is governed by its Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. TypeBound connects to Supabase for cloud accounts; Supabase's privacy practices apply to that service.

Advertising, analytics, and cookies

PsReader does not operate advertising or first-party analytics on the portfolio pages. This does not mean that third-party embeds are cookie-free: the Spotify player may use cookies or similar technologies under Spotify's policies, subject to your browser settings. Typing analytics inside TypeBound are computed locally; TypeBound separately sends race scores to its leaderboard as described above.

Your choices

  • Clear any project's data through your browser's site-data controls.
  • Export and import TypeBound records from the app's backup panel.
  • Ask for your TypeBound cloud account to be deleted.
