Lumen / WCAG contrast guide

Accessibility reference

WCAG contrast, explained clearly.

Contrast ratio is the practical distance between a foreground and background color. Use this guide to understand what the number means and which threshold applies to your interface.

Check a color pair

The four thresholds Lumen reports

AA normal text · 4.5:1

The common minimum for body copy and most interface text.

AA large text · 3:1

Applies to text at least 24px regular or 18.5px bold.

AAA normal text · 7:1

A stricter target for enhanced readability and demanding contexts.

Non-text contrast · 3:1

Controls, focus indicators, icons, and meaningful graphics need enough contrast to remain identifiable. Lumen reports this separately so a passing text pair does not hide a weak control boundary.

Why the ratio matters

Two colors can feel harmonious and still be difficult to read. Lumen compares relative luminance using the WCAG formula, checks the raw value before rounding, and then shows a plain-language result in context.
