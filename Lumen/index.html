How to read the results

AA normal text is the common minimum for body copy. AA large text applies to text at least 24px, or 18.5px when bold. AAA is a stricter target for enhanced readability. Non-text covers controls, icons, focus indicators, and meaningful graphics.

Built around the WCAG relative luminance formula. Compare raw values; round only for display.
