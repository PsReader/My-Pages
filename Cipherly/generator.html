Why this is secure ＋

Cryptographic randomness. Cipherly uses your browser’s crypto.getRandomValues(), designed for security-sensitive randomness rather than predictable pseudo-random numbers.

No memory by design. Results stay in this tab. Cipherly has no account, analytics, password history, or server upload.

Best practice. Use a password manager, keep one password per service, and prefer passphrases when you need something memorable.
