Overview

The Everlasting Pathway provides abilities of knowledge-based spells, mentally sense others by thought and voice therefore affecting them, directly implant knowledge, pull philosophy to influence many on a thin line between wisdom and madness, and eventually become subordinate spiritual entities of corruptive ravings. "They" hold authority over Consciousness and Spirit.[1]

In the Mental domain, the Everlasting Pathway is stronger than the Visionary Pathway.[2]

This Pathway is one of the new Pathways introduced in Circle of Inevitability. It exists outside the twenty-two Standard Pathways, but is still classified into Sequence 9 to Sequence 0.[3]

On Earth, the Beyonders of this Pathway do not advance through Potions, but by praying to the Outer Deity Inextinguishable Ravings, who is the Above the Sequence of this Pathway, for a Boon. The corresponding Sefirah is the Inextinguishable Ravings's currently unnamed Sefirah.

This Pathway is a mix of two different Pathways:[4][5]

  • The Pathway of Philosophy.
  • The Pathway of Consciousness and Spirit. The Sequence 0 name is Everlasting.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Awakens the Original Creator’s spiritual imprint within Sefirot, Uniquenesses, and Beyonder characteristics, leaving exposed beings vulnerable to madness.
  • Projects a voice directly into protected spiritual structures, bypassing Marionettes, Avatars, and Afterimages to implant intent.
  • Manifests as frenzied thoughts or formless consciousness, producing direct mental corruption and disordered spirits.
  • Injury to the deity can spread chaotic consciousness across a region or star domain, while philosophical revelations inflict physical and mental anguish on listeners.

Corruption from Inextinguishable Ravings

The Inextinguishable Ravings is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Known corruptive effects

  • Awakens the Original Creator’s spiritual imprint within Sefirot, Uniquenesses, and Beyonder characteristics, leaving exposed beings vulnerable to madness.
  • Projects a voice directly into protected spiritual structures, bypassing Marionettes, Avatars, and Afterimages to implant intent.
  • Manifests as frenzied thoughts or formless consciousness, producing direct mental corruption and disordered spirits.
  • Injury to the deity can spread chaotic consciousness across a region or star domain, while philosophical revelations inflict physical and mental anguish on listeners.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Inextinguishable Ravings. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
