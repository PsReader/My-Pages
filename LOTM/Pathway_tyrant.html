Tyrant Pathway

Sequence 9: Sailor → Sequence 0: Tyrant

The Tyrant Pathway focuses on water and weather, combining water, wind, electricity, underwater capability, flight, disabling song, and anger-fueled power. At high Sequences it extends to sea-creature command, ocean dominion, earthquakes, light-speed travel, immense electricity, and fear or oppression.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Overview

Pathway themes & abilities

Its recurring domains are sea, land, and air, with abilities centered on water, wind, weather, electricity, aquatic life, and calamity. The pathway also emphasizes wrath, intimidation, and horror: users can instill or resist fear and weaponize voice, storms, and thunder.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Cataclysmic Interrer: Cataclysmic Interrer summons hurricanes, tsunamis, earthquakes, downpours, floods, lightning chains, windblade legions, and true flight.
  • Sequence 3 — Sea King: Sea King commands marine life and seas, walks seabeds, raises tsunamis, creates lightning storms, and unleashes destructive water droplets.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Calamity: Calamity gains city-destroying storms, tsunamis, and cold, plus terror-inducing sound, magnetic manipulation, electromagnetic disruption, and grief-laden singing.
  • Sequence 1 — Thunder God: Thunder God becomes near-light-speed energy and commands thunder, wind, and calamities through heavenly punishment, Storm Hell, and Ocean of Lightning.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Tyrant: Tyrant commands matter, thunder, water, wind, calamity, and intimidation, converting energy and unleashing vast electromagnetic storms and disasters.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for both novels. The Tyrant’s Sequence 0 is the Lord of Storms, whose church controls this pathway. The deity was Leodero, formerly the Ancient Sun God’s Wind Angel. Leodero joined Rose Redemption, whose conspiracy eventually led to the Ancient Sun God’s death, and rose to Sequence 0 afterward. Alger Wilson’s modern arc links the storm church to the Sea God faith in the Rorsted Archipelago: he left the storm church to become the Sea God Church’s masked pontiff, later attained Sea God status, and by the end of Circle of Inevitability had switched to the Hanged Man pathway

Story connections

  • Leodero’s succession: Wind Angel under the Ancient Sun God, member of Rose Redemption, then Lord of Storms after the Ancient Sun God’s death
  • Alger’s church realignment: Alger left the Church of the Lord of Storms and became the masked pontiff of the Sea God Church in Rorsted; he later became its Sea God and eventually switched pathways
  • Bayam conflict: In the 1350 World War, the Lord of Storms fought alongside the Evernight Goddess against the God of Steam and Machinery in Bayam; their side won

Figures to know

  • Leoderoformer Wind Angel and the Lord of Storms, Sequence 0 of Tyrant
  • Alger Wilsonformer Church of the Lord of Storms operative and Tarot Club’s Hanged Man; later Sea God Church pontiff and Sea God
  • Ancient Sun GodLeodero’s former master; his death is tied to Rose Redemption’s conspiracy
