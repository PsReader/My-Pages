Wheel of Fortune Pathway

Sequence 9: Monster → Sequence 0: Wheel of Fortune

The Wheel of Fortune Pathway, also called the Fate Pathway, represents the possibility of fate and Chaos. Its Above the Sequence and Sefirah are both the Key of Light, and its Beyonders can travel freely in the River of Fate.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its central authorities are Fate, Probability, Cycle, and Disorder, supported by Good Luck, Bad Luck, Foresight, Save, and Select. The progression moves from danger-sensing and divination to deliberate calamity, blessings, misfortune fields, prophecy, fate connections, reincarnation, and rebooting. Rather than winning through raw force, the pathway changes the conditions around an event until the desired outcome becomes the most likely—or the only surviving—possibility.

High-sequence abilities

These are the defining capabilities that separate the upper pathway from its lower-Sequence foundations. Details contain major spoilers.

Misfortune Mage

The saint-level pathway user can create misfortune fields, strengthen life-threatening curses, sense changes in fate, and counterbalance bad luck with blessings that push probability toward survival.

Chaoswalker and Soothsayer

These levels read and redirect disordered fate. They can cleanse spiritual contamination, trace fate through dreams or objects, reveal possible outcomes, and bestow either fortune or misfortune on a target.

Snake of Mercury and Wheel of Fortune

At the angelic and divine peak, Cycle and Reboot can trap events in loops, restore a saved state, or force a new run through fate. Sequence 0 embodies Fate and controls Probability, Cycle, Disorder, and the Light Cone World.

Paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries. The Wheel of Fortune, also called the Monster or Fate pathway, is associated with the Key of Light and with fate, probability, and cycles. In the Fourth Epoch, after Ouroboros hunted the God of Luck, Will Auceptin rose as an Angel; later, anticipating Ouroboros’s attack, Will restarted as Will Ceres and became an ally who repeatedly aided Klein. Ouroboros had been the Ancient Sun God’s Angel of Fate and joined Rose Redemption; after the Sun God’s fall, Ouroboros led the group and followed the True Creator

Story connections

  • Will and Klein: Klein’s dream communication with Will begins after he acquires Will’s folded paper crane; Will later helps him with the Die of Probability and other situations
  • Will versus Ouroboros: Ouroboros’s predicted attack forces Will to restart as a child, keeping their long-running conflict personal as well as supernatural
  • Ouroboros and Klein: In Calderón City, Ouroboros investigates after the True Creator’s attention is drawn and tries to stop Klein with a Reboot

Figures to know

  • Will Auceptin / Will CeresWheel of Fortune Beyonder, President of the Life School of Thought, and Klein’s ally; his restart as Will Ceres sets up his conflict with Ouroboros
  • OuroborosAngel of Fate and one of the Ancient Sun God’s Kings of Angels; later a leader of Rose Redemption aligned with the True Creator
  • Ancient Sun GodOuroboros’s former lord; Ouroboros’s past affiliation with Rose Redemption is tied to the Sun God’s fall
