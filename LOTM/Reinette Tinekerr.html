Character profile

Reinette Tinekerr

Chained · Sequence 2: Ancient Bane (Chained Pathway)

Reinette Tinekerr is Klein Moretti's messenger, who asks for one gold coin per message. "She" is a Beyonder of the Prisoner Pathway and was a leader of the Rose School of Thought.
