The Apocalypse Doctrine is a Secret Organization worshipping the Monarch of Decay.

Among the Outer Deities, only the Mother Goddess of Depravity and the Mother Tree of Desire were successful in establishing a unified world-spanning faction of their own inside the Earth's barrier. The other Outer Deities were unable to achieve that, so they proceeded by establishing organizations of varying sizes, each seeded in different regions. The Apocalypse Doctrine is one such organization that worshipped the Monarch of Decay.

Source note: Information from the Lord of the Mysteries Fandom Wiki (WeChat posts by Cuttlefish That Loves Diving).
