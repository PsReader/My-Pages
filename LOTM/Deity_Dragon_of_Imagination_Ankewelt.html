Ancient God

Ankewelt

Overview

Other Identities: Ariehogg, Alzuhod

Regarding the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt, there was a legend that whatever he imagined would manifest. The kingdom he imagined would definitely descend upon the material world. The future it declares would definitely play out and become reality.

Derrick Berg, Book 1, chapter 238

The Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt is one of the eight Ancient Gods from the Second Epoch. "He" was an Ancient God of the Visionary, and the White Tower. "He" also had authorities over the Paragon.

Appearance

In the sea, an even bigger grayish-white dragon spanning a thousand meters long rose up. One of “His” vertical pupils was pale gold and the other was bright red color. There was a third eye on the dragon’s forehead. It seemed to be hiding a thick shadow.

Book 1, chapter 1343

"His" appearance was influenced by Chaos Sea corruption.2

Pathway Composition as an Ancient God

"His" Pathway composition is as follows:3

  • Visionary: Uniqueness + 2 Sequence 1 + 1 Sequence 1 (Envisioned).
  • White Tower: Uniqueness + 1 Sequence 1.
  • Paragon: 1 Sequence 1 (Later separated) + 1 Sequence 2
  • Hermit: Unknown4

"He" was initially called an Ancient God because "He" had Sequence 1 characteristics from other Pathways aside from the adjacent Visionary and White Tower. Later on, "He" gradually gave birth to children to expel the Sequence 1 characteristics from other pathways — killing all except the child that became the Dragon of Nightmare. "His" state then could be defined as a True God; however, because "He" still appeared quite mad on the surface, everyone else considered "Him" an Ancient God, as others couldn't see the detailed composition of "His" characteristics.1Author's WeChat post ancient_gods, part 9

  • "He" expelled 1 Paragon Sequence 1 through giving birth to children.5
  • "His" Sequence 2 Paragon characteristic has Sefirah contamination. "He" could have expelled it, but to resist the malignant aspects related to the Sefirah, "He" needed some corruption from the Paragon side to help.6

History

According to the Myth of Creation, the dragons were formed from the brain of the Original Creator.7

"He" was born in the mid First Epoch.8

In the elves' chronicle, during the Early Era of Fire of the Second Epoch, the non-humans races fought against the quasi-humans races, devils and demonic wolves. This corresponds to the records of the City of Silver. "He", the Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace, and the Mutated King Kvastir were on one side, fighting together against the Giant King Aurmir, the Elf King Soniathrym, the Sanguine Ancestor Lilith, the Devil Monarch Farbauti, and the Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea.9

According to records of the City of Silver, "He", along with the other Ancient Gods, all lost "Their" godhood after the Ancient Sun God awakened and reclaimed "His" authorities.

Only the Forsaken Land of the Gods still remembered the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt. The rest of the world has already forgotten "His" existence; recently, the god of the Visionary Pathway had been replaced by the Visionary Adam.

Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt had conjured a floating city with massive temple pillars supporting a towering palace complex. The name of the city was Liveseyd, meaning the City of Miracles. It disappeared and became a myth. However, Klein, Audrey, and Leonard found its traces in the Groselle's Travels.

Resurrection Plan

The specialty of Visionary Pathway allowed Ankewelt to be able to create Alzuhod with "His" Sequence 1 Author Beyonder characteristic and one of "His" identity or a certain lifeform that’s ‘Envisioned.’ However, Ankewelt had been corrupted and had characteristics of other pathways, putting "Him" in a poor condition, unable to separate the 2nd sequence 1 Beyonder characteristic without losing control. Nonetheless, through certain methods, Alzuhod would become Ankewelt.10

Alzuhod

Ankewelt planned the meeting of the Quill of Alzuhod and Groselle's Travels in the real world. The meeting would awaken the Quill, i.e. a Sequence 1 Author Beyonder Characteristic. Then combined with the negative personality sealed behind the bronze door in the Liveseyd in Groselle's Travels, Ankewelt would be able to use the power of Chaos Sea and become one of the strongest King of Angels. Such a foundation would have given "Him" a chance to compete with Adam for the position of and completely revive, but if "He" resurrected using only the Quill of Alzuhod and tried to manipulate things using "His" negative personality, "He" would be in a very dangerous situation because there is a deep connection between the negative personality and Chaos Sea. Thus, the negative personality is a bit stronger than "Him," there would be all kinds of issues.11

This plan failed because of Adam's interference.

Ariehogg

Based on Ariehogg's reaction and subtle change after hearing about Liveseyd, it is suspected that "He" is another identity of Ankewelt.12
