Sealed Artifacts

Grades, abilities, drawbacks, and statuses from the artifact archive

An archive of dangerous power

Sealed Artifacts offer powerful effects at the cost of significant drawbacks. Browse the local records by grade without leaving the archive.

Sealed Artifacts are items that offer a wide variety of powerful effects at the cost of a significant drawback. The drawbacks occur because sealed artifacts are in a state of "having lost control." They are either man-made or natural.

Their powers often correspond to certain sequence abilities, such as the Blood Vessel Thief 's correspondence to the Error Pathway 's Sequence 6 Prometheus . They often have abilities of the lower sequences too, like the Hand of Horror which has abilities from sequences 7 , 6 and 5 of the Black Emperor Pathway .

Although Sealed Artifacts ’ main powers and negative effects mostly adhere to the Sequence traits of the 22 pathways , just like how people have different personalities, each Sealed Artifact is unique. It might be due to fusing with an object, the environment when formed, the possible existence of a High-Level’s aura, or a curse from the original owner; all kinds of strange traits manifest as a result. No one can imagine the different permutations and list them all out.

Because they can augment the user with abilities outside of their own sequence, they are very valuable and prized; sometimes sold for exorbitant prices, if at all. However, some negative effects might stack and synergize into something worse, which makes it dangerous to transport several of them.

Sealed Artifacts usually come about in one of two ways: as a result of the death of an out of control Beyonder, or through craftsmanship by a Sequence 6 or higher of the Paragon Pathway . A few, like the Biological Poison Bottle , are a result of an experiment gone horribly wrong. In each case, they require a Beyonder Characteristic or the main material of a pathway potion to form; hence all sealed artifacts, if purified or destroyed, will result in the original Beyonder Characteristic or main material. The downside of the sealed artifact could be reduced depending on the sequence level of the Artisan who crafted it, or with the power of a Sequence 4 or higher of the Death Pathway , but there will always be some of downside.

When a Beyonder Characteristic combines with the environment to form a Sealed Artifact , the spirituality used to influence the outside world come from the Spirit World and itself.

If someone has an affinity with a Saint/Holy Artifact (Sequence 4 or above), it can reduce the negative effects by deceiving it to a certain degree. The so-called affinity is the similarity between one’s psyche and the remnant High-Sequence Beyonders psyche mark on the Sealed Artifact .

Many mystical items have rather obvious side effects that can cause certain harm. Therefore, the seven orthodox Churches would seek them out and seal them. They’re called Sealed Artifacts and are distinguished using numbers as code names. Eye of Wisdom On Lord of Mysteries , Chapter 257

Grade 0

33 entries

Extremely dangerous, most important, and most confidential. They are not to be inquired, disseminated, described or spied on. Their abilities and power level are equivalent to an Angel (Sequence 1 or 2 Beyonder). Although Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts are numbered in order of appearance, the Churches already possessed a large number of Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts before the numbering procedures was established in the late Fourth Epoch and early Fifth Epoch . As such, for the first portion of sealed artifacts, the ones with smaller serial numbers are usually more powerful or terrifying than their higher numbered counterparts. Then, the rest were labeled based on the order of appearance.

The more powerful a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact was, the more terrifying the negative effects would be. It was even more so for those that could produce effects at the level of a deity. Even a Sequence 0 true deity might not be able to withstand it for too long. Therefore, true deities wouldn't unseal them unless it was necessary; otherwise, this would bring about an even greater disturbance to "Them," causing them to suffer a net loss.

According to the rules of the Church of the Evernight Goddess , they can only be sealed in the basement of the Holy Cathedral.
