Sublunary Eye Pathway

Outer Deity: High-Dimensional Overseer · Boon advancement

The Sublunary Eye, also called the Painter or Pixie pathway, centers on territory creation, true sight, painting, imagination, reality, and dimensional manipulation. Its users create mystical paintings and worlds, materialize fiction, manipulate strings, and alter the dimensionality of targets. The High-Dimensional Overseer is its Great Old One and source of Boons.

Non-standard pathway lore and Outer Deity corruption details contain major story spoilers.

Notes




High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Freely traverses higher and lower dimensions and changes the dimensionality of other beings, countering effects such as parasitism, puppetry, and teleportation.
  • Reduces targets into paper-like or painted forms that merge with scenery and lose ordinary freedom of movement.
  • Creates painting-worlds with their own suns, planets, rules, and principles, then traps powerful enemies inside them.
  • Within its paintings the Overseer can govern reality with near-total omnipotence and create painted entities capable of True God-level destruction.

Corruption from High-Dimensional Overseer

The High-Dimensional Overseer is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Known corruptive effects

  • Freely traverses higher and lower dimensions and changes the dimensionality of other beings, countering effects such as parasitism, puppetry, and teleportation.
  • Reduces targets into paper-like or painted forms that merge with scenery and lose ordinary freedom of movement.
  • Creates painting-worlds with their own suns, planets, rules, and principles, then traps powerful enemies inside them.
  • Within its paintings the Overseer can govern reality with near-total omnipotence and create painted entities capable of True God-level destruction.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to High-Dimensional Overseer. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
