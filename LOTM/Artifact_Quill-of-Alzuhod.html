Quill of Alzuhod

Grade 0 · Code 0-08

Overview

Quill of Alzuhod (0-08) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: A common quill.

Function

  • Can make what it writes happen if it is possible and without the participants knowing, except if they notice the coincidences.
  • The more you know about 0-08, the more it knows about you and everyone around you.

Downside

Tries to kill user, causes a backlash when used.

Status

Destroyed

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
