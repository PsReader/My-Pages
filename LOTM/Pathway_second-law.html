Overview

The Second Law Pathway revolves around using diseases, decay, and pathogens to affect others and bring about inevitable death. It specializes in spreading pathogens and diseases, corrupting others and organizations, using certain death and sure hit attacks, and survivability, excelling at self-division and replication. At High-Sequences, "They" have authorities over Time, Senescence, Disease, Decay, and Entropy.[1][2]

This Pathway is one of the new Pathways introduced in Circle of Inevitability. It exists outside the twenty-two Standard Pathways, but is still classified into Sequence 9 to Sequence 0.[3]

On Earth, the Beyonders of this Pathway do not advance through Potions, but by praying to the Outer Deity, the Monarch of Decay, who is the Above the Sequence of this Pathway, for a Boon. The corresponding Sefirah is the Monarch of Decay's currently unnamed Sefirah.

TRANSLATION NOTE
Even though the Sequences names of "Child of Decay", "Left Hand of Decay", and "God of Decay" all use the term "Decay" in the WebNovel English translation, in the original Chinese novel, they use different words with different nuances.
  • Child of Putrefaction (腐烂之子, Fǔlàn zhīzǐ):
    • This word denotes rotting and turning sodden / mushy with liquid.
    • This is the same word used in the description "the source of putrefaction腐烂: 
      Fǔlàn      " of the Pathway of Disease疾病: 
      Jíbìng      .
  • Left Hand of Decay (衰败左手, Shuāibài zuǒshǒu):
    • This word denotes weakening / declining / waning / deteriorating into ruin.
    • This is the same word as the "Decay" in "Monarch of Decay" (衰败君王, Shuāibài jūnwáng, lit. 'Monarch of Decline')
    • This is the same word as the "Decay" in the Pathway of Decay衰败: 
      Shuāibài      .
  • God of Senescence (衰老之神, Shuāilǎo zhī shén):
    • This word denotes senescence / weakening with age.
      • The first character (衰, Shuāi, lit. 'weaken/decline') is also the first character in the "Decay" of the "Monarch of Decay" (衰败, Shuāibài).
      • The second character (老, Lǎo, lit. 'old') denotes old age.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Causes planets, ecosystems, and living beings to decay, rot to death, or transform into loyal and disgusting creatures.
  • Destabilizes the internal balance of powerful beings and makes failure, collapse, and corruption increasingly likely.
  • Drags a region’s time toward its conclusion, slowing surrounding events while hastening the approach of an endpoint.
  • Uses Certain Death and Sure Hit symbolism to make fatal attacks or unavoidable decay into supernatural certainties.

Corruption from Monarch of Decay

The Monarch of Decay is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Known corruptive effects

  • Causes planets, ecosystems, and living beings to decay, rot to death, or transform into loyal and disgusting creatures.
  • Destabilizes the internal balance of powerful beings and makes failure, collapse, and corruption increasingly likely.
  • Drags a region’s time toward its conclusion, slowing surrounding events while hastening the approach of an endpoint.
  • Uses Certain Death and Sure Hit symbolism to make fatal attacks or unavoidable decay into supernatural certainties.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Monarch of Decay. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
