Aurore Lee

Hermit Pathway · Sequence 7: Warlock

Aurore Lee is a transmigrator, writer, and foster sister of Lumian Lee in Circle of Inevitability. She is a member of the Curly-Haired Baboons Research Society, where she uses the codename “Muggle.”

This article contains major spoilers for Circle of Inevitability, including events in Cordu.

Background

Aurore came from modern China and awoke in the body of Roche Louise Sanson. She later settled in Cordu Village and took in Lumian, a homeless boy. She writes novels and works as a columnist for Novel Weekly.

Pathway

Aurore is a Sequence 7 Warlock of the Hermit Pathway. The body she transmigrated into had previously advanced along the Eternal Aeon Pathway. These are distinct histories and should not be read as one sequence progression.

See the archive’s Pathways reference for pathway summaries.

Cordu

Aurore’s story in Book Two is tied to the events that destroyed Cordu Village. A conflict between her and the Roche Louise Sanson persona grew after a soul-summoning spell brought Roche’s memories and personality to the surface. During the ritual in Cordu, Aurore regained control long enough to push Lumian away from the altar, but the ritual still killed her and the villagers present.

Later, soul fragments of Aurore and the other villagers were sealed inside Lumian by the Fool. The story leaves Aurore’s ultimate fate unresolved in the source material summarized here.
