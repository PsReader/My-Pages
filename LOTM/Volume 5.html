Volume 5: Red Priest

Quote

"Every drowning person will struggle." — Cuttlefish That Loves Diving

Overview

Red Priest is the fifth volume of the Lord of Mysteries series.

Title Meaning

Red Priest means war. It represents the struggle of all drowning characters as they face unprecedented challenges and revelations.

Synopsis

Ascension to Divinity

Klein reaches the peak of godhood and learns profound truths about the nature of the world and its hidden history. This knowledge fundamentally alters his understanding of his place in the cosmos.

The Greatest Challenges

Klein faces his most formidable challenges yet as multiple powerful entities converge on Backlund. Ancient forces thought long dormant awaken, and multiple pathways to divinity collide in spectacular confrontation.

Convergence of Powers

As the strongest powers in existence clash, Klein must navigate through a labyrinth of deception, betrayal, and revelation. The fate of the world hangs in the balance as he fights to protect those he cares about while pursuing his ultimate goals.
