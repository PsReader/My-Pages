Eye of Crystal

Grade 3 · Code 3-1328

Overview

Eye of Crystal (3-1328) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 3 Sealed Artifact. Grade 3 artifacts are the lowest sealed class, roughly Sequence 8 to Sequence 9.

It is described as: A monocle.

Function

Allow the wearer to see the spiritual body directly, as well as ghosts and shadows.

Downside

Attract monsters like wraiths and shadows to the vicinity. If it was worn for a long time, one's vision would suffer irreversible damage.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
