Primordial God Almighty

Lord of the Astral World · Father of All Living Beings

Overview

The Primordial God Almighty is the former Great Old One, God Almighty, who was most active on Earth from the Pre-Epoch to the middle of the First Epoch, and the former owner of the Chaos Sea. He is the Lord of the Astral World.1

Not to be confused with the God Almighty, the honorific used for the God of the Abrahamic faiths.

Honorific Name

God Almighty, Creator, Maker, the Omnipotent and Omniscient, Lord of the Astral World.2

Appearance

The Primordial God Almighty's full appearance has never been shown, but his consciousness inside other beings has manifested in the forms below.

  • Eternal Blazing Sun: a colossal luminous humanoid figure surrounded by chaos.3
  • Lumian Lee: a vortex-like face with no eyes, nose, or mouth, no bones, composed entirely of a chaotic liquid embodying every color.4
  • Malevolent Dragon: a head that was a swirling amalgamation of liquid, encompassing all colors and possibilities. At its facial features (eyes, nose, ears, and mouth), holes had been drilled, one or two in each location.5

History

Pre-Epoch

In a time more ancient than the Modern Era of the Pre-Epoch, the sleeping Original Creator had naturally split into multiple personas. They were also in a state of slumber, but they began to use the authority and Beyonder Characteristics they controlled to influence the world, making many preparations for the true dissociation which would occur when the Original Creator's body awakened. Among the multiple personas, the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings were the most powerful and active on Earth.6 During this time, the Primordial God Almighty's holy temple was in the West.7

In this era, there was a myth: the God Almighty of Heaven created a divine child. Several Angels, led by an Archangel, rebelled. They were defeated, cast into the Abyss, creating Hell, and turning them to devils. This myth might never have actually occurred; instead, it represented a tendency, a connection between Heaven and the Abyss.8

Research of the appearance of oil in a dried-up oil field. Why would they need to build a research facility in such an unimaginable place for such matters? God, what did they discover deep in the oil field. This is some amazing material. What exactly happened? The doctor turned into a puddle of black oil in front of me. More and more people have turned into oil. This research facility has been sealed from the outside. No one can leave. No one can leave. Mad, they've all gone mad. We're still normal, but our food is almost running out. I seem to be hearing things. There seems to be sounds coming from underground. It's summoning me. "He" is summoning me.

Note from a researcher in Chernobyl, Book 1, chapter 1222.

The underground corruption from the Chaos Sea (and thus, the Primordial God Almighty) had always been influencing the human world in an unobvious manner. It can intermittently bring about certain mysterious events. Since oil itself is a product derived from the Chaos Sea's corrosion of underground regions,9 when the Chernobyl research facility probed deeper into the dried-up oil field and discovered some strange material, the Original Creator fully awakened, turning people into oil and causing them to go mad, summoning them to the underground.10 As a researcher from Chernobyl, the Ancient Sun God fell into the Chaos Sea, and would only awaken in the Second Epoch.

First Epoch

Lost Era

After the Original Creator awakened and split apart, the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy leveraged their long-prepared ancient schemes across the times of the Old Earth to gradually gain advantages in their fight against the Sefirot. The two Pillars eventually defeated the other Sefirot (excluding the Brood Hive and the Key of Light) and shattered their wills, making those wills take a very long time to recover and awaken again.11

After the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy absorbed their first additional Sefirah, they exceeded the limit of the Pillar, the highest level of stability one can achieve. This made their inclination to converge more intense, making it impossible for them to control themselves, and they began taking actions against each other.12 The devouring of their first additional Sefirot marked the end of the Lost Era.13

  • The Primordial God Almighty first accommodated the City of Calamity because of a mystical connection, an instinctive tendency that had long existed. Beyond his deep desire for it, even though he was unaware of the setup and contingency plans left by the Original Creator to stabilize existence through the Mirrored Original Creator,14 he likely foresaw the dangers. Thus, he intervened personally by inserting his own consciousness into the City of Calamity as a Chaotic Head to act as a barrier to prevent yin and yang from fully merging and forming the Mirrored Original Creator.15
  • Afterwards, he accommodated the Tenebrous World because, already under the convergence instinct, he prioritized the association between Heaven and the Abyss, which was also consistent with the mystical similarity between the degenerate nature of the Hanged Man and the Abyss Pathways.16

Era of Destruction

Some time in the mid-First Epoch, foreseeing parts of the future, the Primordial God Almighty and the Brood Hive gave birth to the twins Omebella and Zedus.17 Both the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy made various arrangements, some to ensure their own revival, and some to prevent the other's revival.18

  • The Primordial God Almighty also had undisclosed backup plans related to the Western Continent.19

After the Celestial Worthy completed the creation of the Magic Wishing Lamp, he and the Primordial God Almighty engaged in full-scale war, extinguishing the sun and plunging the world into darkness.20 No one, not even the Seven Lights of the Spirit World, knows the exact situation of the battle between the Celestial Worthy and the Primordial God Almighty. After the battle, both Pillars perished.

  • The Primordial God Almighty's corpse formed the First Blasphemy Slate in the Chaos Sea, and beside it was the Uniqueness of Error,21 which was intentionally used by the Celestial Worthy to stop the Primordial God Almighty's first resurrection within the Ancient Sun God.22
  • The Celestial Worthy sealed all Sefirot apart from Sefirah Castle and the Chaos Sea to the Western Continent.23

Before their deaths, the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy's strong and still perfect and whole power deterred the Outer Deities, who could only settle for observing Earth from afar for the time being. During this era, the Primordial God Almighty served as the sun. After his death, the world was cast into darkness until the ascension of the Ancient Sun God.24

At some point in the First Epoch or Second Epoch, Edefana was polluted by the Chaos Sea, then influenced and driven by the godhood of the Primordial God Almighty. This was the reason why he was able to survive to the Fifth Epoch.25

Second Epoch

According to Sasrir, not long after the Ancient Sun God walked out of the Chaos Sea in the Second Epoch, he sensed that the Primordial God Almighty was awakening inside his body. Therefore, he deliberately split a portion of his persona, fusing the Uniqueness of the Hanged Man and its authorities of Degeneration with the Hanged Man Pathway's Beyonder Characteristic, creating Sasrir to control the Chaos Sea and to isolate it from his true body to prevent any contamination and corruption. Later, he made another attempt to fight against the awakening of the Primordial God Almighty in him, by having children to decrease his Beyonder Characteristics, leading to the birth of Adam and Amon.26 But in the end, the Primordial God Almighty still awoke in his body.

Third Epoch

Just as the Ancient Sun God and his faction were about to win against the Evernight Goddess, God of Combat, and Earth Mother completely, he began to have problems with the revival of the Primordial God Almighty inside himself. This was why he convened the various Kings of Angels and Subsidiary Gods with the Evernight Goddess to establish the Rose Redemption to assassinate himself, so that after reviving and escaping the Primordial God Almighty, he would truly gain control of the Chaos Sea and the corresponding five pathways. Ultimately, the Ancient Sun God was successfully killed, but his revival plan failed because of the betrayal of the three Kings of Angels.27 The Ancient Sun God was unable to foresee the betrayal of the three Kings of Angels because of interference from the Primordial God Almighty.28

Because the Eastern Continent was the most prosperous at the time, one of the reasons for the destruction of the Eastern Continent was that the Primordial God Almighty wanted to reduce the number of anchors of the Ancient Sun God.29

Fourth Epoch

In the First Epoch, the Primordial God Almighty left another revival plan in the two pathways of Calamity. When the Chaos Demoness combined with the Red Priest, he would become the Trinity, in the City of Calamity and within the Chaos Demoness and the Red Priest, simultaneously resurrecting, ultimately forming a whole, returning in the mirror. But he perished too early, and his arrangements were not as expected, despite the union of the Primordial Demoness and Alista Tudor in the Fourth Epoch. The City of Calamity was sealed in the Western Continent by the Celestial Worthy, preventing him from quickly becoming the Trinity. In addition, there was also another change on the City of Calamity's side. His consciousness revived in the Primordial Demoness and Alista Tudor, but without the City of Calamity's resonance and linkage, that consciousness grew very slowly, and was suppressed and controlled by the two. With the combination of the initial feminine and masculine sides of the Original Creator symbolized by the Chaos Demoness and the Red Priest, the consciousness of the Primordial God Almighty, and the Special Mirror World created by Bethel Abraham who was influenced by the godhood of the Celestial Worthy, the development of things deviated from the Primordial God Almighty's arrangement, and led to the consciousness of the Mirrored Original Creator to be born, though his consciousness was very weak, like all newborn infants.30

Fifth Epoch

The Fool's Dream

In The Fool's Dream, the five figures of "Peng Deng", "Grisha", "Nie Zhen", "the boss of Aurora Company", and "Zhang Yujia" corresponded to (not in order) the five identities: the Ancient Sun God, the True Creator, Adam, the Primordial God Almighty, and Sasrir.31

  • "Peng Deng" and "Grisha" (Peng Deng's roommate) symbolized Adam and the Primordial God Almighty, but their specific positions are not fixed. They can swap at any time, symbolizing fusion and confrontation.
  • "Nie Zhen" (Peng Deng's girlfriend) symbolized Sasrir, representing the part that split off from the Ancient Sun God. Adam's rib is his Eve.
  • "The boss of Aurora Company" symbolized the True Creator. The True Creator and Adam had already achieved consciousness fusion, so they don't need to share rent anymore. They can act under different identities through The Fool's cognition, but their thoughts are interconnected.
  • "Zhang Yujia" (Zhou Mingrui's and Peng Deng's childhood friend, who once sent Zhou Mingrui a postcard with an image of the Corpse Cathedral) symbolized the Ancient Sun God. The Corpse Cathedral itself belonged to him. Zhang Yujia wandered outside the dream city, symbolizing that the Ancient Sun God had already left this stage. Now his successors and the Primordial God Almighty were confronting each other.

The Primordial God Almighty was one of the people who least wanted the Celestial Worthy to resurrect. As Peng Deng, he was the previous renter of the Magic Mirror Arrodes from the Star Dream Provisions Store since the birth of that magic mirror was related to him,32 presumably to set up the trap regarding the Mirrored Original Creator to the Celestial Worthy later on.

He did various actions to help Lumian's team to oppose the Celestial Worthy. He appeared as Grisha and specifically arranged to guide Lumian to discover the corresponding psychological trauma of Klein in the underground area of Mushu Hospital. Later on, he appeared as Peng Deng when talking with Anthony and gave him the hint that he was not only Zhou Mingrui's childhood friend, but also the Celestial Worthy's childhood friend.33

As a top-tier Dreamweaver, the Primordial God Almighty also helped Medici, who already had a corresponding manifestation in the dream city, to come into the Dream.34

The Day of Many Disasters

After The Hostel incident,35 the Primordial God Almighty made a contract with the Mother Goddess of Depravity: he would help her bypass the barrier and reclaim the Brood Hive and the corresponding Uniquenesses and Beyonder Characteristics, and she would help him revive. In addition, the Mother Goddess of Depravity also entered a contract with the Mother Tree of Desire, witnessed by the Primordial God Almighty, where the Mother Tree of Desire would sacrifice the Tree of Shadow to help the Mother Goddess of Depravity descend on Earth in The Day of Many Disasters. In return, the Mother Goddess of Depravity would need to give assistance to the Mother Tree of Desire later.36

During The Day of Many Disasters, the Primordial God Almighty had two chances to resurrect. The first chance was when Lumian was forced to fuse with a split-off self from the Primordial Demoness, the mirrored Alista Tudor, both the masculine and feminine aspect of the Mirrored Original Creator, and an Apocalypse and Conqueror characteristic. The balance was lost with Jenna's advancement to Demoness of Unaging, which attracted the feminine Mirrored Original Creator away from Lumian, before it was regained with the addition of the Mother's powers37 via Omebella's bloodline and Zedus's vitalized soul fragments inside Lumian. After the balance was regained, Lumian's body became the resurrection cornerstone for the Primordial God Almighty, who manifested as a Chaotic Vortex Face. The Primordial Demoness was also forced to retreat to resist against the Primordial God Almighty's reviving consciousness. The Chaotic Vortex Face was eventually sealed by The Fool using the dark-golden mask taken from Loki.38

The second chance happened during Omebella's birth. Since the Primordial God Almighty was Omebella's father, her birth strengthened the symbolism of the Father on the Eternal Blazing Sun, causing him to lose control of the fragile balance. This attempt was thwarted when the Eternal Blazing Sun voluntarily chose death at the hands of the Evernight Goddess.

During the event, Naboredisley also experienced the revival of the malicious consciousness and frenzied spirit of the Primordial God Almighty because he had accommodated the Tenebrous World in the First Epoch. However, the revival of the Primordial God Almighty was short-lived, with his consciousness retreating to the Chaos Sea, leaving only his malicious spirit behind, preventing a true balance to be reached with the Mother Tree of Desire's corruption, though it did improve Naboredisley's state.39

The Primordial God Almighty retracted his powers and influence over Edefana to focus on his two resurrection opportunities, causing Edefana to age and eventually die.40

Come, my child. Merge with me. You are out of time. The apocalypse is nigh. Your only choice is to risk merging with me, then contend for dominance on equal footing.

Primordial God Almighty to Grisha Adam, Book 2, chapter 1115.

Lastly, the Primordial God Almighty successfully persuaded Grisha Adam to enter the Chaos Sea and merge with him, as the Mother Goddess of Depravity would soon descend onto Earth through the strengthened symbolism of Omebella's birth, and time was running out.41 This gave Grisha Adam enough strength to cooperate with The Fool to create the Protected Zones to save Earth from the initial impact of the Mother Goddess of Depravity's descent on Earth.42

Before The Apocalypse

In the next one year when the Mother Goddess of Depravity was chasing and accommodating the Brood Hive within the gray fog sealing the Western Continent, Grisha Adam and the Primordial God Almighty were locked in a fierce struggle, with the Primordial God Almighty gaining the upper hand.

When Lumian went to meet with Grisha Adam, he removed the dark-golden mask originated from the Celestial Worthy, which gave the Primordial God Almighty a choice. If he diverted his focus to influence Lumian's head and face to resurrect there, symbolically, it would mean abandoning the contest with Grisha Adam, causing him to gain back the advantage. If he refused to retreat or give up, the revival of Lumian's head and face would stagnate, yet their unique qualities will remain intact, allowing Lumian to make use of their powers. The Primordial God Almighty chose not to revive on Lumian.43

The Apocalypse

When The Apocalypse began, Grisha Adam merged with the Uniquenesses and Beyonder Characteristics from the Lord of Storms and the God of Knowledge and Wisdom to temporarily achieve a fragile balance using their remnant psyche.44 This gave him the strength of a Quasi-God Almighty and allowed him to hold off the Primordial Hunger for the duration of the battle. However, during the battle, Grisha Adam was first affected by the Inextinguishable Ravings,45 and then the Mother Goddess of Depravity's New Life,46 which strengthened the consciousness of the Primordial God Almighty and made his resurrection imminent, even when put into a slumber by the Eternal Darkness.47

Towards the end of the battle, Klein proposed a temporary truce to the Mother Goddess of Depravity. He modified the contract drafted by the Primordial God Almighty, which was originally exceptionally tight and without exploitable Loopholes, into producing one: "The time limit is until the conclusion of this event". As the Mother Goddess of Depravity hadn't yet reclaimed the Uniqueness of Mother and Moon, nor achieved all her objectives, the current event wasn't considered over, allowing her to retreat and return back at a later time.48

After the battle finished, Grisha Adam accepted his death at the hands of Lumian Lee to stop the awakening of the Primordial God Almighty. After the Primordial God Almighty was killed, he hadn't tried to resurrect within Lumian, as adding anything else to Lumian's body would render even the most powerful and genuine Angel of Redemption unable to redeem the situation.

After the Primordial God Almighty's near resurrection in The Apocalypse, his consciousness had significantly strengthened. Without hundreds or thousands of years to wear it down, none of the God Almighty's five pathways are suitable for birthing a corresponding Sequence 0.49

Trivia

  • It is strongly implied that the Primordial God Almighty corresponds to the God of the Abrahamic faiths.
    • The term God Almighty (上帝, Supreme Thearch, Shàngdì) in Chinese refers to the absolute god of the universe. It is commonly used as a non-religious translation for the Abrahamic God.
    • With regards to the Chaos Sea, in biblical literature, the sea frequently symbolizes chaos and disorder. In Genesis 1:2, the sea is depicted as a primordial abyss over which the Spirit of God hovers. This imagery underscores the sea as a symbol of the chaotic forces that God orders and subdues.
    • The novel mentioned the Pre-Epoch myth of the God Almighty of Heaven creating a divine child, who, as an Archangel, led several Angels in rebellion before being defeated and cast into the Abyss, creating Hell and turning them to devils. This myth likely refers to the story of Lucifer.
    • The author confirmed that the Primordial God Almighty accommodating the City of Calamity corresponds to the God of War and Storm, the Lord of Armies YHWH in Canaanite mythology.50
