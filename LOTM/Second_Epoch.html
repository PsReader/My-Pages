Second Epoch

The Dark Epoch—an age of Ancient Gods, rival civilizations, and humanity’s long struggle for freedom.

The Second Epoch is remembered as humanity’s Dark Epoch. Dragons, Giants, Elves, Sanguines, and other Beyonder races rule the world, while humans live as subordinates or slaves. The source chronology divides the epoch into five eras and ends with humanity’s revolt in the Dawn Era.

Major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries and related lore follow.

The eight Ancient Gods

During the Early Era of Fire, eight Ancient Gods divide the remaining powers and authorities of the Creator. The source describes them as rulers of the sky, land, oceans, Spirit World, Astral World, and the various races. Each has subsidiary gods drawn from the Angel sequences.

Sprouting Era Years 0–1,268

As creatures of chaos dwindle, a more recognizable age of civilizations begins. The Giant King, Elf King, Mutant King, dragons, phoenixes, demonic wolves, sea races, and demons contend for dominance while other Ancient Gods gradually emerge. The source notes that Giants, Elves, Vampires, and Humans are still understood as unified races rather than the later divisions.

Early Era of Fire Years 1,268–1,730 · Age of Giants

The eight Ancient Gods rule a world divided among competing racial powers. Accounts describe human-like races fighting non-human peoples, devils, and demonic wolves. Human societies become more established, but humans remain beneath the Giants, Elves, and Sanguines. The source also describes a secret struggle and betrayals among the Ancient Gods. It later clarifies that Lilith was not truly dead and secretly took Omebella’s place as Goddess of Harvest.

The source explains the name “Dark Epoch” by saying the Supernova Dominator occupied the sun, while the Earth’s Barrier prevented its light from reaching the world.

Dual Era Years 1,730–1,960

The First Blasphemy Slate reveals the divine pathways. The source describes humans fracturing under pressure from other races, while Giants and others increasingly regard themselves as divine rather than human. After Lilith, Kvastir, and Flegrea perish in a conspiracy, distrust between the Giants and Elves erupts into the 230-year War of the Betrayers. Dragons and demons intervene, while the forces of Aurmir and Ankewelt become the dominant powers reflected in the era’s name.

Era of Tranquility Years 1,960–2,325

Giants, Elves, Dragons, Demons, and Phoenixes reach a stalemate, creating 365 years of relative peace. Enslaved humans lose much of their memory of pre-cataclysm civilization. The source describes younger Giants and Elves beginning to show more emotion during this period.

Aurmir’s household works with human secret organizations in an effort against Soniathrym. The source suggests that one of those organizations may have worshipped the Ancient Sun God.

Dawn Era Years 2,325–2,561

Public creation myths and City of Silver records describe humanity’s revolt as the work of the gods or of a waking Creator. The source’s account of the underlying history identifies Adam, who called himself God Almighty, as the figure later known as the Ancient Sun God. In Year 2348, he kills the Elf King and Dragon of Imagination. The Giant King and Phoenix Ancestor are severely wounded, while the Devil Monarch sacrifices much of his power to seal the Abyss.

Humanity becomes favored by the Omniscient and Omnipotent God and begins studying mysticism. The Pathway system, including potion formulas and rituals, takes shape; Hermes creates the Hermes Language. The source places the Evernight Goddess, Earth Mother, and God of Combat’s Sequence 0 ascensions near the end of this epoch.
