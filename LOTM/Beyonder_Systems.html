Beyonder Systems

Characteristics, potion formulas, and sequence advancement materials

Power has a price

Every pathway is built from Beyonder Characteristics, potion formulas, and the risks of accommodating supernatural power.

Potion (魔药, Mó yào ) refers to the mixture of the Main Ingredients (the Beyonder Characteristic ) and other Supplementary Ingredients according to the correct formula. Consuming a correctly-concocted Potion will drastically reduce the chance of failures during Advancement compared to consuming a raw Beyonder Characteristic directly. If one wants to take the risk, the chance of success isn't too low below Sequence 5.

The concoction of a Sequence potion is extremely simple, at least for Sequence 7 and below. There's no need for a special flame or any additional ritual, much less an incantation. There's no need for one to participate in it spiritually. All one needs to do is go according to the formula's steps, add the precise amounts, and mix it. That will be all.

In the process, there are Main Ingredients and Supplementary Ingredients . The sole purpose of supplementary Ingredients are to reduce the negative effects of the Beyonder Characteristic and the corresponding mystical influence. The exact amount of supplementary ingredients doesn't significantly impact the final result. While the quantity can be slightly reduced, it must not deviate too much from the requirement, or the advancement might fail. Moreover, exceeding the specified quantity, even by a small margin, could lead to the need for mental health treatment, and immediate death is not out of the question.
