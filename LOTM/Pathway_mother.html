Mother Pathway

Sequence 9: Planter → Sequence 0: Mother

The Mother Pathway centers on life, plants, and earth, with additional emphasis on healing, genetics, and creating new lifeforms from existing ones. At high sequences, it can accelerate growth, create chimeras, humans, and constructs, drain life to return it to earth, and support rebirth and life creation.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its documented themes include botany, healing, crossbreeding, biological mutation, chimera and construct creation, alchemy, fertility and reproduction, desolation, and life manipulation. At Sequence 0, its core authorities are Mother, Earth, Nature, parts of Death and Life, and subordinate authorities including Desolation, Healing, Transmutation, Return, and Harvest.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Ancient Alchemist: Mutates bodies and minds, creates artificial life and humans, commands low-intelligence creatures, heals severely, and gains supernatural transformation.
  • Sequence 3 — Pallbearer: Commands death and return: raises evil spirits, drains life, returns undead to earth, and seals undead angels.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Desolate Matriarch: Exercises earth, desolation, harvest, and life over broad areas; creates constructs, toxins, evil spirits, and mythical transformations.
  • Sequence 1 — Naturewalker: Reshapes environments, merges with natural elements for travel or power, and creates conceptual nature realms.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Mother: Embodies Mother, Earth, Nature, partial Death and Life; births deities, concepts, worlds, mutates life, and enables return.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for both novels. The Church of the Earth Mother is an orthodox institution founded in the Third Epoch, and its clergy control the Mother and Moon pathways; its faith centers on the Earth Mother. A late revelation recasts that identity: Lilith, the Second-Epoch Sanguine Ancestor believed dead, survived by taking the fate and identity of the Giant Queen Omebella, then eventually ascended as Sequence 0 Mother. In the Third Epoch, Earth Mother joined Evernight Goddess and God of Combat in opposition to the Ancient Sun God’s faction, and later participated in Rose Redemption, a major conspiracy that led to his death

Story connections

  • The stolen identity and delayed apotheosis: Lilith faked her death, assumed Omebella’s identity with help attributed to Evernight and the Ancient Sun God, and waited for Badheil’s apotheosis before advancing to Mother
  • A hidden role in the Third Epoch’s power struggle: Earth Mother allied with Evernight and God of Combat against the Ancient Sun God’s faction and later joined Rose Redemption
  • The church under pressure in Circle of Inevitability: To guard against false revelations from the Mother Goddess of Depravity, the church split its Beyonders into the Favored and the Blessed; the Day of Many Disasters then devastated the Favored faction

Figures to know

  • LilithSanguine Ancestor, concealed Earth Mother, and Sequence 0 Mother
  • OmebellaGiant Queen whose fate and identity Lilith appropriated; the Silver City’s records preserve an important trace of her story
  • Evernight GoddessLilith’s ally in the concealment scheme and a partner in the Third Epoch coalition
  • Emlyn WhiteSanguine and high-ranking deacon of the Church of the Earth Mother
