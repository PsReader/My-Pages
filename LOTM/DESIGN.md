# LOTM archive design read

Reading this as a novel reference archive for LOTM readers, Victorian records room editorial style, dials E2/R2/M1.

- Color: the original crimson moon image sits under a purple veil, with rose-brown text and gold accents.
- Layout: the crimson moon hero gives the page a clear entry point, followed by searchable records and grouped sections. Sticky navigation keeps the main destinations in reach.
- Type: Georgia keeps the page lightweight and gives headings an editorial character without a remote font dependency.
- Spacing: the hero has room to establish the subject, while tighter archive rows keep browsing efficient.
- Cards: square catalog rows keep titles, emblems, and descriptions easy to scan. Search filters the visible entries and volumes as the reader types.
- Art: the existing crimson moon background, LOTM emblems, and thumbnails supply the identity; no new assets or unsupported claims are added.
- Scope: the wiki remains full novel and can contain spoilers. Each content page carries a warning. Chapter based spoiler filtering needs source content tagged with reveal chapters.
