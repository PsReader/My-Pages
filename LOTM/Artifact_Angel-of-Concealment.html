Angel of Concealment

Grade 0 · Code 0-17

Overview

Angel of Concealment (0-17) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: An expressionless woman with black eyes.

Function

  • * Erases people.
  • Descent vessel of the Darkness Pathway Deity.

Downside

Cannot be controlled. The range of abilities randomly fluctuates.

Status

Released from 'Secret' Seal
