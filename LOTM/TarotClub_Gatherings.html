Tarot Club - Gatherings

1. Overview

Tarot Club meetings are mainly held on Mondays at 3 PM in Sefirah Castle, above the gray fog. From 1349 to 1352, Tarot Club meetings occur every week. But after Mr. Fool went to sleep, gatherings occur only once a month, and there is no convener.

The following is a log of the depicted gatherings of the Tarot Club, organized by volume. Click a volume header to expand its meetings.

A gathering to divide the spoils of war among the Tarot Club members who participated in Hunting the Hidden Sage.

  • Mr. Fool informs the gathering of the rewards that will go to their allies in the battle. The Uniqueness of the Hidden Sage will go to the God of Steam and Machinery. The Knowledge Emperor Beyonder characteristics containing Sequence 9 to Sequence 1 will go to Bernadette, which is also a reward for her assistance in The Fool's Dream. The Church of the Evernight Goddess will receive the Destined Madness Grade 0 Sealed Artifact and the Beyonder characteristics of Seids, who was corrupted by this Sealed Artifact.
  • Cattleya receives a Sage Beyonder characteristic as a reward for fulfilling her commission from the Fool.
  • Lumian receives the Weather Warlock potion formula and the Grade 0 Sealed Artifact, Post-Apocalyptic Scripture.
  • Fors receives the Grade 0 Sealed Artifact, Solomon's Mask.
  • Mr. Fool considers the death of the Hidden Sage his reward.
  • Alger receives Merumu's Spellbook.
  • Audrey receives a Grade 1 Sealed Artifact formed by a Manipulator Beyonder characteristic.
  • Sharron receives Eye of Omniscience.
  • Leonard receives Creator's Hammer, planning to use it to create a self-playing piano.
  • Several Hermit Pathway Beyonder characteristics are to be given to the members of the Curly-Haired Baboons Research Society who assisted.
  • The remaining Mid- and Low-Sequence Beyonder characteristics and mystical items obtained are split between Seven of Cups, Two of Cups, and the Church of the Fool.
  • Lumian asks why the Hidden Sage came to life because of the Great Mother. Mr. Fool says only that the problem lies in the West Continent.

Chapters 1087-1092 · 1359

Participants: Klein Moretti, Fors Wall, and Lumian Lee

A mini-gathering, owing to only a few members being Angels at this point.

  • Fors asks Mr. Fool to explain godhood. Mr. Fool explains the concepts of True Godhood, Apparent Godhood, and the Vague Conception of Godhood that many people have.
  • Mr. Fool also explains high level concepts, including Above the Sequence, the goals of the Great Old Ones, the Original Creator, the history of the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings and God Almighty, the Sefirot, the Pillars, and the theoretical Fourth Pillar.
  • Fors advises Lumian on being an angel, while also revealing her preparations to advance to Key of Stars. She and Lumian worry that her status as a Sequence 1 will make her a target during the Apocalypse.
Volume 8: Eternal Aeon

Chapter 1128 · 1359

Participants: Klein Moretti, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Derrick Berg, Fors Wall, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, and Sharron

A small meeting conducted to announce to the other Major Arcana the Chariot Lumian Lee's intention to devastate the Demoness Sect. Lumian is not present.

  • Mr. Fool instructs Sharron that Reinette Tinekerr is also to participate.

Chapters 1142-1143 · 1360

Participants: Klein Moretti, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Derrick Berg, Fors Wall, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, Sharron, and Lumian Lee

A meeting to divvy up the spoils of war from the Death of Primordial Demoness and the last meeting before The Apocalypse.

  • While Lumian lacks many valuable artifacts to reward the other members for their participation, he promises to aid them whenever they need. The other members see this as more valuable, now that he is a dual-pathway God.
  • Lumian promises to trade Farbauti's Abomination characteristic to Sharron for the Calamity of Crimson. He then offers to trade Calamity of Crimson to Derrick in exchange for Gift of the Land. If he can resolve its issues, he offers it to Audrey, who in turn offers to lend it to Fors, to help with her plan to relocate the Abraham family to another planet.
  • Lumian offers Fors the chance to travel the Special Mirror Worlds he now controls, in exchange for spreading faith in him. He also shares his new honorific name.
  • Lumian proposes his help to Cattleya and Bernadette in digesting their potions.
  • Lumian also tells Leonard to tell Pallez Zoroast that he owes him a favor.
  • Lumian asks Fors to borrow the Box of the Great Old Ones to resolve the issues on its third level.
  • Xio trades Solomon's Mask to Leonard for Pillar of Severity. She also asserts that she won't be switching to the Black Emperor Pathway.
  • Mr. Fool suggests that since the Book of Calamity task assigned to Alger cannot be completed before the Apocalypse, he instead switches to a neighboring pathway to become an angel sooner. He offers characteristics from the Sun Pathway, White Tower Pathway, or Tyrant Pathway in exchange for The Setting Sun Rooster. Alternatively, he suggests shattering and purifying the Setting Sun Rooster to obtain the characteristic for Profane Presbyter. Alger accepts the latter proposal, deciding to finally live up to his Hanged Man codename in full.
  • Emlyn asks for suggestions on digesting his High Summoner potion. Lumian offers to allow himself to be summoned.
  • Mr. Fool congratulates them all on the success of the operation and remarks that there is about a month left before the Apocalypse. He hopes they will all be present at the next Tarot Meeting.

Chapter 1179 · 1360

Participants: Klein Moretti, Audrey Hall, Alger Wilson, Derrick Berg, Fors Wall, Emlyn White, Cattleya, Leonard Mitchell, Xio Derecha, Sharron, and New Major Arcana: Franca Roland

The Empress Franca Roland's first depicted gathering, although she joined previously. She has replaced Lumian, since he is now a God.

  • At Mr. Fool's request, Fors, with help from the others, recounts how she and the Tarot Club, barring Audrey, Emlyn, and Leonard, visited the planet Lorbath, along with Pallez Zoroast, Hela, Reinette Tinekerr, and Aurore Lee. The natives worship Supernova Dominator, and they met the God of Steam and Machinery, who is interested in the local technology. Mr. Fool listens with a smile and doesn't interrupt.
  • Last Tarot Club meeting.
