Twilight Giant Pathway

Sequence 9: Warrior → Sequence 0: Twilight Giant

The Twilight Giant Pathway is a traditional warrior pathway combining offensive and defensive abilities: extreme strength, weapon mastery, supernatural resistance, Dawn-light weapons and armor, protection, and high-sequence alchemy and concealment. Its Above the Sequence is Eternal Darkness, and its corresponding Sefirah is the River of Eternal Darkness.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its core authorities are Twilight, Combat, Holiness, and Proxy, with Decay and the Passage of Time as symbols. Progression emphasizes combat mastery and physical growth before adding Dawn purification, protection, alchemy, concealment, mercurial liquefaction, aging and decay, and deity-mediated power.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Demon Hunter: Identifies weaknesses and corruption, crafts alchemical effects, conceals intentions, and strengthens protection and Dawn powers.
  • Sequence 3 — Silver Knight: Liquefies into mercury, manifests teleporting Silver Rapiers and Light Concealment, and materializes powerful Silver Armor.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Glory: Uses simplified Twilight, Combat, and Holiness authorities; forms sealing Cages, Twilight Swords, and annihilating light storms.
  • Sequence 1 — Hand of God: Channels a pledged deity's power through Proxy, briefly lends marks, and strengthens Protection with radiant Cages.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Twilight Giant: Wields Twilight, Holiness, Combat, and Proxy; causes decay, aging, purification, devastation, protection, and time-slowing effects.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for Lord of Mysteries and Circle of Inevitability. The Twilight Giant pathway’s “twilight” motif carries historical weight: its Sequence 0, the God of Combat, was once Badheil, eldest son of Giant King Aurmir and Goddess of Harvest Omebella. In the Second Epoch he served Aurmir; later he joined the Rose Redemption faction whose rebellion helped bring down the Ancient Sun God. Much later, the God of Combat’s killing by Evernight and Lilith ended the 1350 World War. In the Forsaken Land, Colin Iliad led the City of Silver; he died restraining Sasrir at Giant King’s Court so Derrick could open a path to the outside sunlight

Story connections

  • Ancient succession and betrayal: Badheil inherited divine power after Aurmir fell, became the Twilight Giant Sequence 0, and joined Rose Redemption; in the Fifth Epoch his death at Evernight and Lilith’s hands concluded the 1350 World War
  • A city enduring the darkness: The City of Silver survived 2,582 years in the Forsaken Land after the sun vanished. Its people found a route out through Giant King’s Court, then established the New City of Silver, which became a Church of the Fool holy land
  • Colin’s final sacrifice: Chief Colin Iliad, a Twilight Giant demigod, died restraining the Dark Angel spirit Sasrir during the last investigation of Giant King’s Court. His death gave Derrick time to open the gate; Colin’s final revelations included the City’s “Gift of the Land” curse

Figures to know

  • BadheilGod of Combat: Former God of Dawn and Aurmir’s eldest son; eventually became Sequence 0 of Twilight Giant and was killed by Evernight and Lilith
  • AurmirGiant King: Ruled the Kingdom of Silver and passed his power to Badheil after his fall
  • Colin IliadCity of Silver chief: Twilight Giant Sequence 3 Silver Knight, responsible for his city’s survival and central to its exit from the Forsaken Land
  • Evernight Goddess (Amanises)One of the two deities who killed the God of Combat; the other was Lilith, acting under the guise of the Earth Mother
