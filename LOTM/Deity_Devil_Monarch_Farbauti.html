True God

Farbauti

Overview

Other Identities: Farbauti, Naboredisley

‘He’ was originally the only surviving Ancient God, the Devil Monarch, Farbauti. Now, heh.

Amon to Klein Moretti, Book 1, chapter 1152

The Devil Monarch Farbauti is a Survivor from the Modern Era and one of the Ancient Gods from the Second Epoch. "He" was originally an Ancient God of the Abyss and the Wheel of Fortune, and later a True God of the Abyss.1Author's WeChat post ancient_gods, part 27 In addition, "He" has a Sequence 1 Abomination Beyonder characteristic that was later given to Naboredisley, "His" other identity.2Book 2, chapter 697

"He" was the only surviving Ancient God from the Second Epoch in "Their" original form. In later epochs, "He" was known as the Dark Side of the Universe and resided in the Abyss.8 "He" was worshiped by the Blood Sanctify Sect and Devil Families.

Later on, "He" was corrupted alongside the Chained God Tolzna by the Mother Tree of Desire. "He" restored lucidness during the Fifth Epoch before The Apocalypse.3

Honorific Name

As a Sequence 0, Farbauti possesses an unknown Honorific Name.

Pathway Composition as an Ancient God

"His" Pathway composition is as follows:9

  • Abyss: Uniqueness + 3 Sequence 1.
  • Wheel of Fortune: Uniqueness.
  • Chained: 1 Sequence 1.

According to Madam Magician, "He" was born with a Sequence 1 Abomination Beyonder characteristic from a neighboring pathway, making "His" state one of the best among the eight Ancient Gods. "He" had also become the only Ancient God alive in "Their" original form. In fact, if Farbauti were to gather the ingredients of the Abyss Pathway as a Sequence 1 Abomination and complete the deity-ascension ritual to advance, "He" would be equivalent to a True God now—a stronger True God. "He" wouldn't be an Ancient God described by words like madness, cruelty, violence, and bloodlust. However, as the Devil Monarch, even if "He" had done so, "He" would inevitably exhibit the characteristics of the Abyss, experiencing strong influence from this pathway. Therefore, Farbauti is also one of the two or three most unhinged Ancient Gods. This doesn't contradict "His" better state.

Later, "He" sacrificed the the Uniqueness of Wheel of Fortune to escape from the Ancient Sun God in the Second Epoch.5Author's WeChat post ancient_gods, part 31 Afterwards, because "His" remaining characteristics were only the neighboring Abyss and Abomination, even without an Apotheosis Ritual, "He" would be considered a crazy True God.1

At some point, "He" gave the Sequence 1 Abomination Beyonder characteristic to Naboredisley, "His" other identity.2

History

According to the Myth of Creation, "He" and the rest of the devils were formed from the stomach, intestines, and the other evil parts of the body of the Original Creator.10

The Blood Sanctify Sect worship "Him" as the Dark Side of The Universe, they believe believe that "He" is the ruler of the Abyss and that "He" is the devastator of the world who would corrupt and degenerate the real world's entire universe.11

In truth, "He" was a remnant of the Pre-Epoch, a survivor after the Original Creator woke up. When "He" recovered fragments of thought and memory, "He" was already a great devil. Those fragments were soon drowned out by madness and blood lust, only returning near the end of the First Epoch.6Book 2, chapter 1125 "He" became one of the eight Ancient Gods of the Second Epoch afterwards.7Book 1, chapter 313

Among the Ancient Gods, the Devil Monarch Farbauti and the Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea were independent beings who wanted to subvert all order and corrupt all living beings.12

According to records of the City of Silver, "He", along with the other Ancient Gods all lost "Their" godhood after the Ancient Sun God reclaimed "His" authorities from the Ancient Gods.7 However, the truth is that during the Dawn Era in the Second Epoch, "He" was gravely wounded by the Ancient Sun God. By using the majority of the demon race and "His" own power (including the the Uniqueness of Wheel of Fortune5) as sacrificial tribute, "He" converted the Islands of Beginning into the Abyss, sealing it away from the outside world, and using this method to evade the radiance of The Omniscient and Omnipotent.13

Only the Forsaken Land of the Gods still remembers the Dark Side of the Universe. The rest of the world has already forgotten "His" name and only knows the Dark Side of the Universe as the evil god of the Abyss Pathway.

At some point in the Fourth Epoch, "He" was corrupted by the Mother Tree of Desire since "His" pathway originally belonged to "Her".14 15 For some reason, the Underworld Daoist agreed to "His" request for help, causing the Naboredisley's corpse that contained the Abomination characteristic to slumber in a special dream, while being sealed by Death's mask, thus free from the harrassment of the Mother Tree of Desire.16

During Klein's Apotheosis Ritual, Amon placed the first Blasphemy Slate into the Fog Sea, near the Abyss' entrance. This, combined with the enhanced seal brought about by the corresponding authority of Door and half of the Lord of the Astral World's control towards the world barrier prevents the Outer Deities from being able to have "Their" powers penetrate inside for a short period. Without the influence of the Outer Deities, the Dark Side of the Universe and the Chained God did not wish to interfere in the battle of gods. "They" would rather seize this opportunity to escape "Their" restraints.17

As per the content of the book "A Summary of Rumors and Hearsay Before the Cataclysm" in the New City of Silver's library, the name Devil Monarch Farbauti was mentioned:

After the Devil Monarch Farbauti led the Devils back to the Abyss, occasional rumors persisted of Demons enticing humans. “The Demons operated under the following names: “Beelbubli, Almos, Samael, Lilatan, and Naboredisley. “A high-ranking Demon Hunter speculated that these names conceal the aliases of Devil Monarch Farbauti…

Book 2, chapter 631

The alias of the Devil Monarch Farbauti is Naboredisley. The Abomination form chose to sleep and be a corpse in the dream tomb of Tizamo Town. This will allow "His" corpse to enjoy peace and tranquility, safe from the Mother Tree of Desire's harassment. "He" chose to continue in this dead state until after The Apocalypse.2Book 2, chapter 697

In The Day of Many Disasters, because the Primordial God Almighty nearly resurrected, the Devil Monarch Farbauti experienced the revival of the malicious consciousness and frenzied spirit of the Primordial God Almighty within "Him", because the Primordial God Almighty had once accommodated the Tenebrous World in the past. To a certain extent, it balanced the corruption of the Mother Tree of Desire, allowing "Him" to regain a semblance of peace. However, since the revival of the Primordial God Almighty was short-lived, with "His" consciousness retreating to the Chaos Sea. "He" left only "His" malicious spirit, preventing a true balance. Farbauti remained unable to fully escape the control of the Mother Tree of Desire. However, "His" condition had relatively improved—"He" no longer relied on Lilith's aid.6

Naboredisley and Lumian Lee had agreed to collaborate. The plan was that, when Lumian next performed a deep secret deed and projected his spirit and consciousness into the City of Calamity, he would take Farbauti's mental imprint with him. This will help Farbauti establish a preliminary connection with the Tenebrous World. This is possible because the Primordial God Almighty had once accommodated both the City of Calamity and the Tenebrous World, so the two Sefirot had mutually corrupted each other. Through the Tenebrous World's corruption within the City of Calamity, Farbauti can establish an initial connection. This would also benefit Lumian since Farbauti's mental imprint, fused with the Tenebrous World's corruption, would mitigate related risks when Lumian accommodate the City of Calamity, reducing potential dangers to some extent. If this plan succeeds, Naboredisley's sealed Abomination avatar will die, and Lumian may claim its resulting Beyonder characteristic as his own.6

Before The Apocalypse, when Lumian projected his spirit and consciousness into the City of Calamity, Farbauti directly infiltrated his mind and spirit, and neutralized the Tenebrous World's corruption within the City of Calamity.3Book 2, chapter 1152

When the fight against the Outer Deities first started, the Master of the Shadow Cottage met Farbauti in the Tenebrous World. Both beings were unwilling to accept the fate of being devoured by the Great Old Dominators and decided to cooperate.18 "They" fused together with the Tenebrous World to form a quasi-Father of Devils, the Pitch-Black Fiend.4
