1. The Fool's Prayers

Praying to The Fool allows direct communication with him. If Klein doesn't have time to hear the prayer immediately, he can listen to it later, because the prayers are stored in Sefirah Castle. Klein compared it to "wireless communication" and "offline messages". Outside meetings, it was the main way of communication of the Tarot Club between members, or an intelligence report to The Fool.

"I pray for your attention.
I pray for you to listen.
....
[REPORT]"

1.1 Formal Ritual

A ritual to The Fool based on a modified version of the Luck Enhancement Ritual.

First, the ritual can be informal, in an environment that won't be disturbed. Put four brand new candles on an altar, placed on four corners respectively. It's best if they are candles with a sandalwood scent. Put a loaf of white bread near the candle on the top left corner, a bowl of Feynapotter noodles near the candle on the top right corner, paella by the candle on the bottom left corner, and a Desi pie by the candle on the bottom right corner. Use a silver knife to make a sealed spiritual environment. Mix moon flowers, gold mint, slumber flowers, fingered citron, and rock-rose together, then distill it. Extract it for its essential oil, then pour a drop onto each candle.

The incantation used is The Fool's Honorific Name, alongside the following prayer based off Klein's first observed ritual:

"I pray for your help.
I pray for your loving grace.
I pray for you to give me a good dream.
Moon flower, a herb that belongs to the red moon, please bestow your powers to my incantation.
Fingered citron, a herb that belongs to the sun, please bestow your powers to my incantation."
"If you have any matters to tend to that leave you unable to attend the gathering on Mondays, conduct the ritual and inform me (The Fool). All you have to do is change the line in the incantation 'I pray for a good dream' with the reason."
- The Fool

Audrey Hall's experience:

  1. Cleanse one's body before the ritual by bathing in water infused with a few drops of the ritual's essential oil.
  2. Leaving the bath with a clean and naked body, put on a robe: white for women and black for men.
  3. In the same room, conduct the ritual on an impromptu altar (table). Remember to set up a silver-knife-based sealed spiritual environment for protection, then receive The Fool's verbal answer.

1.2 Impromptu Ritual

"You have to use a simple ritual and recite my (honorific) name whenever you return to the City of Silver. I will notify you in advance for future gatherings. You need not pay too much attention to it on other days, nor do you have to avoid anyone. When you receive my notification, isolate yourself within a thousand heartbeats."
- The Fool to The Sun

This is essentially a response to a prayer. The Fool opted to omit the other steps of the ritual, since it was a plea directed toward "Him". The Tarot Club members habitually follow this ritual version as they participate in more active events.

Klein Moretti Version: pray to The Fool without the need of essential oils or burnt herbs to obtain powers.

2. The Fool's Sacrificial and Bestowment Ritual

Introduced in Volume 2: Faceless. A "delivery service" ritual to The Fool, based off The Sun's initial sacrifice ritual. It was more theoretical, with advice from Azik Eggers. The ritual uses Sefirah Castle as an "air transport" point for the delivery of energies and objects to it. The sacrifice and bestowment was once inspired by anecdotes of Evernight gifting holy items to "Her" believers. Therefore, The Fool desired to let certain materials enter and leave Sefirah Castle, establishing a trade route.

First of all, prepare an altar. There's no need to make it too complicated; it can be very simple and crude, using the dualism method. The only requirement is to engrave or draw "His" symbol, the hidden "Pupil-less Eye" and the mysterious symbol of the partial contorted lines, the same symbol on the back of The Fool's chair. Secondly, follow the normal sacrificial ritual procedure, but there's no need to burn any more additional herbs or apply the holy oil. There's no restriction on time, just chanting The Fool's name is enough. Remember to use ancient Hermes or Jotun to recite this piece of prayer:

"Your devoted servant prays for your attention,
I pray for you to take his offerings.
I pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom."

After finishing the chant, combine the materials that contain spirituality with the natural power created by the incantation, and await The Fool's response for the sacrifice. To receive the object from The Fool using the dualism method, recite:

"Your devoted servant prays for your attention,
I pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom.
I pray for you to give me strength."

The ritual creates an illusory gate above the gray fog, similar to the Door of Summoning (Door of Sacrifice and Bestowment). However, a Sequence 8 Clown's spirituality alone couldn't construct a stable channel, and he could only use Sefirah Castle to complete the sacrifice.

Dualistic Ritual Method: place a candle above the drawn symbol or deity-related object. One candle was placed in the altar's middle, representing the performer making the sacrifice.

Klein Moretti Version: uses Sefirah Castle as a storage facility during his adventures.

Derrick Berg Version: uses a candle engraved with The Fool's secret symbol, and recites the sacrificial ritual incantation without setting up a wall of spirituality.

2.2 Rental Business Model

With Fors Wall introducing the Leymano's Travels rental post, The Fool's Sacrificial and Bestowment Ritual was used to allow rental businesses across the Tarot Club members.

Leymano's Travels: each rental basically starts at 300 pounds. Every additional day is an additional 50 pounds. And when returning it, the notebook needs to have two additional pages of Beyonder powers than when it was rented out. If there's only one blank page or no blank pages when it's rented, one just needs to fill up the full book (38 pages).

Unshadowed Crucifix: until the Crucifix was broken to acquire its The Sun blood, the Grade 1 Sealed Artifact was rented at a negotiated price (later barter price once in Derrick Berg's ownership), to help Tarot Club members expel excess Beyonder characteristics or use the Sun domain to apply to various mysticism actions.

3. The Fool's Secret Deed Ritual

An "exorcism" ritual, based on the ritualistic magic Klein found in Klarman's Book of Secrets, based on the Blood Moon Sacrifice. Its first use was to deal with a clone parasite of Amon attached to Derrick Berg.

The process was to use a material rich in spirituality, preferably the blood of a Beyonder, to write the honorable name of the target of the secret deed on an animal hide, and to draw the corresponding symbols and magic labels. If necessary, the specific circumstances of a given time and place also had to be taken into account. When this was done, the ritual host would set up the altar, pick up the piece of hide, and repeat the honorable name, letting their spirituality seep into the hide, gradually spreading it out, little by little, to form a hidden bond with the corresponding great being, and obtaining the corresponding spiritual experience or help.

It was a ritual destined to the Primordial Moon. Klein replaced the honorable name with his own, and the symbol with the one carved behind the seat of The Fool in Sefirah Castle. Finally, he had to design the corresponding magic label according to the symbols and his mysticism knowledge. This was the most difficult step. Any mistake would cause the entire ritual to produce unpredictable developments. Lastly, he modified the layout of the original altar to make it closer to "The Fool" and to the "King of Yellow and Black".

It could only give knowledge or direct effects such as purification or corruption, letting the target's mind gain a wonderful experience. It couldn't interfere with the divination or other matters done by another person. Simply put, the secret deed ritual directly affected the target's Spirit Body, Astral Projection, Body of Heart and Mind, and Ether Body. The related positive or negative states were removed.

The principle of a secret deed ritual is to adjust one's state, release one's mind and body, and align oneself with the target of the prayer bit by bit. Finally, an overlapping will occur, allowing one to acquire the corresponding knowledge. And this kind of alignment and overlapping is relative. While acquiring knowledge, the performer's secrets will also be open to the target of the prayer.

It can only be performed on the performer's body, and not towards an external object.
