Salinger's Blood Banner

Grade 0 · Code 0-01

Overview

Salinger's Blood Banner (0-01) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: A charred banner. The flagpole is iron-black metal, and there are a large number of dangerous blood spots on the banner.
