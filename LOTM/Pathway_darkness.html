Darkness Pathway

Sequence 9: Sleepless → Sequence 0: Darkness

The Darkness Pathway centers on night and sleep: its users grow stronger at night, can pacify or host souls, enter dreams, induce sleep, see in darkness, and suppress moods and desires. At higher sequences it develops darkness domains, realistic dreams, concealment, misfortune, horror, deathlike repose, and embodiment of its authorities; its Above the Sequence is Eternal Darkness and its Sefirah is the River of Eternal Darkness.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Overview

The Darkness Pathway centers on night and sleep: its users grow stronger at night, can pacify or host souls, enter dreams, induce sleep, see in darkness, and suppress moods and desires. At higher sequences it develops darkness domains, realistic dreams, concealment, misfortune, horror, deathlike repose, and embodiment of its authorities; its Above the Sequence is Eternal Darkness and its Sefirah is the River of Eternal Darkness.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its recurring themes are darkness, silence, sleep and dreams, spiritual bodies, concealment, misfortune, and horror. Core expressions include Nocturnality, Midnight Poems, dream manipulation, Requiem, Spirit Commanding, Night Domain, Concealment, and Darkness authority.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Nightwatcher: Night Domain boosts the user and weakens enemies; concealment, misfortune, spirit control, and dream invasion also expand.
  • Sequence 3 — Horror Bishop: Horror Bishops wield Sword of Darkness, create city-scale Nightmare Worlds, and project an aura of Horror.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Servant of Concealment: Servants of Concealment erase targets from perception, hide secrets and areas, reverse concealment, and create concealed worlds.
  • Sequence 1 — Knight of Misfortune: Knights of Misfortune wield national-scale misfortune and can return from darkness after death, though not truly immortal.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Darkness: Darkness governs concealment, dreams, eternal rest, spirits, misfortune, catastrophe, and horror, including resurrection in darkness.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

The Church of the Evernight Goddess, the institutional center of the Darkness pathway, emerged in the Third Epoch and helped humanity through the Cataclysm; its Nighthawks later worked undercover as local law-enforcement or security teams. Amanises’s rise began earlier: a subsidiary goddess of the demonic-wolf deity Flegrea, she seized the Darkness Uniqueness and a Sequence 1 characteristic amid Flegrea’s fall, then vanished before ascending. In modern Loen, the Tingen Nighthawks’ struggle against a plot involving Ince Zangwill, artifact 0-08, and the True Creator’s Spawn ended with captain Dunn Smith sacrificing himself to save the city

Story connections

  • [Major spoiler] Flegrea’s downfall: Amanises began as one of Flegrea’s subsidiary gods; amid the conspiracy that killed him, she secured the Darkness Uniqueness and a Sequence 1 characteristic, setting up her later rise
  • The Nighthawks on the ground: The Church’s mostly Darkness-pathway Nighthawks worked undercover in local law-enforcement or security roles. Their Tingen team, led by Dunn Smith, is central to the novel’s opening supernatural investigation
  • [Major spoiler] Ince’s defection and Tingen: Former Evernight archbishop Ince Zangwill defected with Sealed Artifact 0-08. His scheme to obtain Saint Selena’s remains and advance into the Darkness pathway caused the deaths of Dunn and Klein; Dunn’s sacrifice stopped the True Creator’s Spawn and saved Tingen
  • [Major spoiler] Church rivalry and consolidation: The Evernight and God of Combat churches were long-standing rivals. After the God of Combat fell in the Fifth Epoch conflict, Evernight’s church took over his church

Figures to know

  • Dunn SmithDarkness-pathway Nighthawk captain in Tingen; he recruited Klein and sacrificed himself to protect the city
