Monarch of Decay

Second Law Pathway · The Irreversible Entropy

Overview

The Monarch of Decay is one of the Outer Deities from the Cosmos that attempted to invade Earth. He is the current owner of the Second Law Pathway.1

He is the leftover material of the Fourth Pillar,2 and together with other conditions, his symbol of Decay might cause the Fourth Pillar to be born early.3

...the Monarch of Decay would naturally cause the entire planet and all living beings to start decaying, eventually either rotting to death or becoming disgusting creatures loyal to him...

Madam Magician to Lumian Lee, Book 2, chapter 1090.

Formerly called the Malady God.4

Honorific Name

The Inevitable Decay of All Things, The Endpoint of the River of Time, The Lord of Rot and Putrescence, The Irreversible Entropy.5

The honorific name shares several phrases with the description of his three pathways in the author's WeChat post. Some nuances are lost in the WebNovel English translation.

Symbols, Authorities and Abilities

He possesses full authority over the Second Law Pathway and the corresponding symbols, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Second Law: authorities of Time, Senescence, Disease, Decay, and Entropy, and the symbols of Decay, Certain Death, and The Endpoint of the River of Time.6
    • This Pathway is a mix of three different Pathways:7
      • The Pathway of Decay (衰败, Shuāibài).
      • The Pathway of Time (时光, Shíguāng).
      • The Pathway of Disease (疾病, Jíbìng).

Termination

  • He is the leftover material of the Fourth Pillar.
    • His Pathways represent the following aspects of the Fourth Pillar:
      • The eventual Decay (衰败, Shuāibài) of all things.
      • The merciless erosion of Time (时光, Shíguāng).
      • Disease (疾病, Jíbìng) itself, the source of putrefaction (腐烂, Fǔlàn).
    • When the formation of the Fourth Pillar starts from the convergence of the Calamity of Destruction and the Eternal Darkness,8 the Monarch of Decay will be converged into and become a part of the Fourth Pillar.
    • As a Great Existence representing Termination (终结, Zhōngjié), he has the confidence to kill another Great Old One in a short time.9

Decay

  • He is the symbol of The Eventual Rotting of All Things (万物终朽, Wànwù zhōng xiǔ).
    • As long as he exists, one will experience Decay and eventually reach the end of their life unless they reach Sequence 0.10
    • He can make the Decay of an afterimage equal to the Decay of the main body.
    • His symbols include "Anything that can go wrong will go wrong".
      • This can manifest in various ways, from destabilizing the internal balance of Great Existences11 to inducing the early arrival of the Termination symbolism.
      • In The Day of Many Disasters, two of his bestowed Left Hand of Decay, one male and one female, were placed as corpse statues in the side entrance of Alista Tudor's Blood Palace in Fourth Epoch Trier. Their purpose was to symbolize one thing: "making anything that can go wrong be most likely to go wrong", which manifested in the increasingly desperate situation facing those within the Astral Barrier and eventually led to the Mother Goddess of Depravity's descent through the Astral Barrier.12

Time

  • He is The Endpoint of the River of Time (时光之河的终点, Shíguāng zhī hé de zhōngdiǎn), being able to drag the region's Time toward its conclusion.13
    • He can slow the surrounding flow of Time and hasten its approach to the terminal point.14
      • He used this to defend against the Calamity of Destruction's Supernovae and Black Holes. The shock waves that erupted from the former dimmed rapidly before even nearing him, Decaying with astonishing speed, while the latter evaporated at an unimaginable pace, shrinking steadily.15
    • He can manifest watery streams of time that were able to slow the flow of the River of Eternal Darkness.
    • He can push his body into the depths of illusory ripples where it seems to be the end of Time, the final point of change. This can delay his prophesied Death until that Endpoint leaving the present unaffected.16
    • Time, when combined with Decay, gave rise to Sureness (必然, Bìrán).
      • Sure Hit (必然命中, Bìrán mìngzhòng) and Certain Death (必然死亡, Bìrán sǐwáng): he can invoke these symbols as an attack through his Left Hand.17
        • He can make the Death of an Avatar equal the Death of the main body.
        • His Left Hand can block an attack from the Eternal Darkness that combines the River of Eternal Darkness with the three Uniquenesses of Twilight Giant, Darkness, and Death. However, doing so would render him immobile for a period of time.

Disease

  • He is Disease (疾病, Jíbìng) itself, the source of putrefaction (腐烂, Fǔlàn).
    • Virus: he holds the symbol of Virus.18
      • Naturally, the symbol of Virus allows the Monarch of Decay to create Avatars, which manifests at the Doomed One Sequence. However, because he inherently bears the symbolism of Certain Death, any separated Avatars will die very quickly.
        • As a result, he can no longer rely on Avatars to assist in combat the way he could at earlier levels.

Appearance

Usual form: watery streams of time emanated from the Monarch of Decay, whose body was the size of a planet, covered entirely in golden plating resembling an alien mummy. Between the gaps of the golden plates, greenish-yellow pus oozed out, capable of slowing the flow of the River of Eternal Darkness and dragging the region's time toward its conclusion. His left hand was not covered by gold. It was riddled with blue veins, rotting flesh, yellow-green blood vessels, and deeply wrinkled skin.19

True form: after shedding the golden plating covering, his body is a heavily decayed, oozing, yellow-green pus-covered body.20

Background

According to the Seven Lights, ever since the Oldest One awakened and split apart, the most powerful Outer Deities in the entire universe, including the Monarch of Decay, gathered around this tiny solar system.

Some of them wish to retrieve their Sefirot and characteristics that had been ripped from them, and were attracted here. Some of them hope to get neighboring Sefirot and high-level characteristics which they can accommodate.21

The Monarch of Decay's goals were either the City of Calamity or the River of Eternal Darkness, though he could only choose one.22

History

The Monarch of Decay was one of the offspring of the Original Creator and the Mother Goddess of Depravity.23

Lumian Lee first encountered believers of this Outer Deity proselytizing in Trier and assaulted one of them. One of his believers would later be murdered by Lumian after he caused the death of the Ruhr.

The Order of All Extinction is an organization which follows this Outer Deity. They participated in Project Vortex.24 Higdon, a Saint follower of the Monarch of Decay, accompanied Voisin Sanson to recover Ludwig Phil on behalf of the Primordial Hunger, and Termiboros on behalf of the Circle of Inevitability. At the end of Project Vortex, both Higdon and Voisin Sanson perished.

In The Day of Many Disasters, two of his bestowed Left Hand of Decay, one male and one female, were placed as corpse statues in the side entrance of Alista Tudor's Blood Palace in Fourth Epoch Trier. Their purpose was to symbolize one thing: "making anything that can go wrong be most likely to go wrong", which manifested in the increasingly desperate situation facing those within the Astral Barrier and eventually led to the Mother Goddess of Depravity's descent through the Astral Barrier.

During The Apocalypse, he was assisted by the Circle of Inevitability and fought against Lumian Lee and Amanises. During the fight, Lumian and Amanises had nearly succeeded three times to wound or eliminate the Monarch of Decay, but each time, the Circle of Inevitability had intervened by either resetting the state through Loop or exerting influence via the symbolism of Inevitability.25

When Lumian began irresistibly merging with the Monarch of Decay and was about to cause the formation of the Fourth Pillar, the Circle of Inevitability intervened by using the Substitution symbolism to replace the Monarch of Decay within the River of Fate's past with an Angel of Redemption characteristic. He then used his true body to replace the avatar, personally entering Lumian's body to face the encroaching power of Termination. Using his Eternal Aeon symbolism, the Circle of Inevitability achieved Redemption. He then replaced his external Sequence 1 avatar to replace his true body once again, sealing it within Lumian's body, while the true body returned to reality.

After being saved, the Monarch of Decay was last seen fleeing through the Astral World, and was pursued by the Circle of Inevitability, who was tracking him using the mystical connection that was established through the previous use of the Substitution symbolism.26

Trivia

  • The Monarch of Decay and High-Dimensional Overseer can devour each other. If one of them succeeds and further accommodates the Uniqueness of Door, they can attain the King of Space-Time symbolism.27
