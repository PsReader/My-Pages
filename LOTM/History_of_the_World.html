History of the World

From the first three days to the Iron Age: a guide to the eras, powers, and turning points that shaped the world.

This page brings together a short history-by-era overview and the relative-year timeline currently available in the source article. The numbered chronology is presented as reported there; it is not an attempt to fill gaps or reconcile every uncertainty in the lore.

This page contains major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries and references to Circle of Inevitability.

The beginning and Pre-Epoch

The first three days

On the first day, the Original Creator awakens and creates the world. Reality, the Spirit World, the Astral World, and Fate are not yet fully separated. On the second day, the Oldest One gives birth to the Mother Goddess of Depravity; the foundations of Reality are established, the Spirit and Astral Worlds separate, and the River of Fate begins to flow. The Mother Tree of Desire and the Son of Chaos also emerge, bringing desire and order—and, in their wake, corruption and shadows.

On the third day, the Oldest One and the Mother Goddess unite. The Mother of All Things gives birth to the Goddess of Fate, after which the fates of beings begin to form patterns and more foreseeable futures instead of remaining in absolute chaos.

Pre-Epoch

After creating the universe, the Original Creator falls asleep. Personalities such as Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings form from the slumbering Creator and begin to influence reality indirectly through their authorities and Beyonder Characteristics. A cataclysm around the modern era destroys the old world; the Survivors and Transmigrators originate from that period.

First Epoch: The Age of Chaos Read the full guide →

The First Epoch’s exact start and end are unknown. Its early world is described as dark, chaotic, and mad. As the Beyonder races gradually regain reason, the First Blasphemy Slate appears. The Western Continent disappears into the gray fog.

Second Epoch: The Dark Epoch Read the full guide →

As Beyonder Characteristics are purified, the major races develop civilizations and eight Ancient Gods rise one after another, ruling the sky, land, oceans, Spirit World, and Astral World. Humanity struggles under stronger races—including Giants, Elves, and Sanguines—until the Ancient Sun God awakens and takes back authorities from the Ancient Gods. Humanity then rises in revolt.

Third Epoch: The Glorious Era Read the full guide →

Following humanity's revolt at the end of the Second Epoch, Adam—known as God Almighty and the Ancient Sun God—unifies the continent and establishes the Orthodox Churches. The Solomon Empire rises to unprecedented glory, codifying the Pathway system under divine order. As God Almighty grows distant, ambition festers amongst the gods and mortals alike, eventually collapsing the age into war.

Fourth Epoch: The Epoch of the Gods Read the full guide →

The Fourth Epoch is born from the collapse of the Glorious Era. Warring Churches, rising and falling empires, the Blood Emperor, the War of the Four Emperors, and the Conflicts of the Seven Gods reduce the Northern Continent to ruin. It ends in the desolate Pale Era, on the brink of the Fifth Epoch.

Fifth Epoch: The Iron Age Read the full guide →

In the Northern Continent, the seven Orthodox Churches and Four Great Nations shape public life while secret organizations operate in the shadows. In the Southern Continent, resistance movements and kingdoms—including the Highland Kingdom and Paz Kingdom—rise before northern invasions destroy them.

Roselle Gustav’s arrival and improvements to the steam engine accelerate industrialization, steamship travel, piracy, and colonial expansion. A world war engulfs the nations of the Northern Continent. As the barrier weakens, the Outer Deities press against reality, and Boon Recipients grow more numerous, placing the world in greater danger.
