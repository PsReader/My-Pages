Blasphemy Slate

First & Second Slates

First Blasphemy Slate

Appearance

An abnormally ancient stone slab, its surface was mottled with the ravages of time.

The language used in the First Blasphemy Slate seems to be the source of all the languages, including but not limited to Jotun, Hermes, Ancient Feysac, and the Southern Continent's Dutanese. Even if someone has never learned the language before, they could understand the meaning at a glance. This language is also used by the Trunsoest Brass Book to write its Laws.

Power

As a product from the remains of the Primordial God Almighty, the First Blasphemy Slate can be used as a medium to control the power of the Chaos Sea. It can also be used to strengthen the Astral Barrier against the intervention of the Outer Deities.

History

The First Blasphemy Slate was originally born from the remains of the Primordial God Almighty in the Chaos Sea but was later pulled by some forces and left the ground before it was complete. When the Ancient Sun God awakened in Chernobyl, "He" gained possession of the First Blasphemy Slate, and used it as a medium to control the Chaos Sea indirectly through Sasrir to isolate its corruption from "His" main body.

Near the end of the Second Epoch, the Ancient Sun God manipulated the First Blasphemy Slate to emerge, revealing the paths of the divine. However, only part of its content was leaked; the remaining formulas remained unknown. With the appearance of the First Blasphemy Slate, humans fractured under the instigation of dragons, mutants, and demons. Giants and others saw themselves as divine creatures, no longer human. The giants and elves split accusing each other of betrayal regarding Lilith's death and waging the 230-year "War of the Betrayers", with dragons and demons intervening three times.

After the Ancient Sun God's death at the end of the Third Epoch, the First Blasphemy Slate was held by Sasrir's Evil Spirit, who was sleeping inside the Giant King's Court.

In the year 1352 of the Fifth Epoch, the Slate was taken by Amon, after the death of Sasrir Evil Spirit in the main hall of the Giant King's Court.

When Mr. Door was trying to return to the physical world, Amon used the First Blasphemy Slate to resist the descent of the Mother Goddess of Depravity.

During Klein's Apotheosis Ritual, Amon threw the Slate at a mountain's peak that tore out from the Fog Sea that was near the Abyss entrance and the Primitive Island in Fog Sea. This, along with the enhanced seal brought about by Amon's authority of 'Door', and the Half-Lord of the Astral World Adam's control towards the Astral Barrier, prevented any Outer Deities from being able to have "Their" power penetrated inside for a short period. This also temporarily cut off the Mother Tree of Desire's influence over the Dark Side of the Universe and Chained God.

After the Slate was used to patch the Astral Barrier, it was eventually destroyed when the barrier shattered during The Apocalypse.

Second Blasphemy Slate

The Second Blasphemy Slate was born after the fall of the Ancient Sun God. It contains the complete pathway researched information for all twenty-two Standard Pathways, including the Potion names (some were revised compared to the First Blasphemy Slate), Potion formulas, and Advancement Rituals. The main difference between the Second Blasphemy Slate and the First Blasphemy Slate isn't that the latter has many unique Sequence names, but that the former had many additions. It was a secret the Ancient Sun God caught a glimpse of towards "His" later years-the secrets of Above the Sequence as fruits of the Ancient Sun God's research.

The knowledge on the Second Blasphemy Slate appeared in its essence and not in textual form, thus anyone who saw it needed some time to interpret it.

Appearance

A gray stone slab, with many mottled marks on its surface, making it look rather ancient. It was very similar to the First Blasphemy Slate, but it didn't have that abnormally ancient feeling to it. It also had words that looked like the source of all languages.

Power

As a product from the remains of the Ancient Sun God who was a Quasi-God Almighty and almost equivalent to the owner of the Chaos Sea, similar to the First Blasphemy Slate, the Second Blasphemy Slate can be used as a medium to control the power of the Chaos Sea.

History

When the Ancient Sun God perished, "He" used a portion of "His" body and will to create the Second Blasphemy Slate, to bring light for The Apocalypse by ending the chaotic battle between the deities due to anger, emotions, and other reasons, initiating the era of working towards becoming Great Old Ones. During its creation, the Primordial God Almighty interfered by altering the content of the Sequence 0: Demoness' Apotheosis Ritual to aid "His" resurrection plan.

After emerging, the Second Blasphemy Slate was moving around and was seen by many people, most of them later established Angel Families in the Fourth Epoch: Bethel Abraham, Solomon, Zaratul Senior, Tudor, Trunsoest, Augustus, Einhorn, Sauron, Castiya, Zoroast, Stiano and Constantine. According to Bethel Abraham, Cheek and the Artisan had also seen the Slate with "Him". Because the Slate might leave at any moment, and because the knowledge on it needed time to be interpreted, most of them could only memorize at most one complete Pathway. Adam took the Slate in the end.

The Twilight Hermit Order under Adam then controlled the Second Blasphemy Slate. As a member, Emperor Roselle had seen the Second Blasphemy Slate and through it, created the corresponding Cards of Blasphemy. However, the version that he saw is incomplete and missing some information. This missing information is later revealed to be the secret of Above the Sequence.

The Fool had seen the Second Blasphemy Slate when he was captured by Adam. He managed to read the information on The Nine Great Old Ones on Earth, the demigod potion formula information of the Visionary, Darkness, and Justiciar pathways, and likely all of the remaining contents.

Later, Adam arrived in the Forsaken Land of the Gods to confront the True Creator so "They" could fuse and "He" could be whole again. Adam used Arrodes to trigger the Chaos Sea, then "He" used the Second Blasphemy Slate to form a connection with the triggered area. "He" then stepped forward into the shadow curtain to confront and merge with the True Creator.

During Project Vortex in 1359, Adam uses the Second Blasphemy Slate as a medium to send Chaos Sea to severely injure the Uncertain Mist through the Genie and Roselle, ensuring one less danger during The Apocalypse.

Historical Figures & Epochs

World history

History

The Beginning

On the first day, the Original Creator awoke and created this world, but Reality, the Spirit World, the Astral World, and Fate were still mixed together.

On the second day, the Oldest One birthed the Mother Goddess of Depravity. Reality gained a foundation, the Spirit World and Astral World were separated, and the River of Fate began to flow. Soon after, the Mother Tree of Desire was born, giving rise to life and introducing evil and desire. Then came the Son of Chaos, who established order-and with it, shadows naturally emerged.

On the third day, the Oldest One and the Mother Goddess united, and the Mother of All Things gave birth to a firstborn daughter, the Goddess of Fate. From then on, the fates of all things began to intertwine, no longer in absolute chaos but instead forming relative patterns and foreseeable futures.

Pre-Epoch

Main article: Pre-Epoch

After the Original Creator created the universe, "He" fell asleep. The slumbering Original Creator spontaneously split out multiple personalities such as the Primordial God Almighty and the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings. They are also in a state of deep sleep, but they have begun to use their own authority and Beyonder Characteristics to indirectly and secretly interfere with and influence the real world, making many preparations for the true division after their initial awakening.

The cataclysm came around the modern era and everything was destroyed. During this time, the stars and the earth all experienced great changes. The Survivors and Transmigrators came from this era.

First Epoch: The Age of Chaos

Main article: First Epoch

Unknown when it started, unknown when it ended.

The beginning was dark, chaotic, and filled with madness until Beyonder races eventually gained some rationality. The First Blasphemy Slate first appeared in this epoch. The Western Continent disappeared in the gray fog.

Second Epoch: The Dark Epoch

Main article: Second Epoch

Due to the purification of Beyonder Characteristics, the major races have evolved their own characters and civilizations, and eight Ancient Gods have emerged one after another. "They" ruled sky, land, and oceans. "They" ruled over the Spirit World and Astral World, and various races.

Humanity struggled to survive and became subordinates or even slaves of Giants, Elves, and Sanguines. Until the Ancient Sun God woke up and took back the authorities from Ancient Gods, allowing humanity to successfully rise up in revolt.

Third Epoch: The Cataclysm Epoch

Main article: Third Epoch

After the death of all Ancient Gods, the Ancient Sun God became the absolute ruler of the world and the one and only Orthodox God. Humans walked out of the shadow of other races and became the dominant race of the land. They started to study the world of mysticism. They created the Pathways system with potion formulas and rituals based from the First Blasphemy Slate.

Soon later, the Ancient Sun God found the awakening of the Primordial One in his mind and attempt fight against "Him". Under the tacit agreement, the Rose Redemption was founded. This led to the fall of the Ancient Sun God, and the Second Blasphemy Slate was created from "His" remains after "His" death.

The Lord of Storms, Eternal Blazing Sun, God of Knowledge and Wisdom and True Creator were born afterward.

Fourth Epoch: the Epoch of The Gods

Main article: Fourth Epoch

After the fall of the Ancient Sun God, the Eastern Continent where the City of Silver was located was isolated from the rest of the world, and became known as the "Forsaken Land of the Gods".

The Solomon Empire established, led by the Black Emperor Solomon with support from the True Creator, as well as the Rose Redemption.

Later on, Alista Tudor and Trunsoest rebelled against the Black Emperor Solomon and the Solomon Empire with the support of the six gods. They successfully killed the Black Emperor and established the Tudor-Trunsoest United Empire, replacing the Solomon Empire.

Eventually, the Black Emperor Solomon managed to resurrect and rebuild the Solomon Empire. This led to the great war: War of the Four Emperors.

Fifth Epoch: The Iron Age

Main article: Fifth Epoch

After the fall of four emperors. The Northern Continent is ruled by seven Orthodox Churches and the Four Great Nations, and there are also many secret organizations operating secretly.

In the early days, in the Southern Continent, due to the fall of the Underworld Emperor, the Resistance gradually appeared at the sea. The Highlands Kingdom and the Paz Kingdom were founded, before they were destroyed by the invasion from the Northern Continent nations.

The Industrial Revolution started with the transmigration of Huang Tao as Roselle Gustav. The improvement of steam engine cultivated the growing number of pirates and the birth of steamship. Then started the era of Colonialism.

In the Year 1350, the world war occurred embroiled all of the nations of Northern Continent.

In the Year 1358, Outer Deities penetrated the barrier more and more intensely, and the number of Boon Recipients who pursued mysterious power and believed in evil gods continued to increase. The end was approaching, and the world was in a greater crisis.
