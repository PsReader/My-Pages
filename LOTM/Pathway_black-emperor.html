Black Emperor Pathway

Sequence 9: Lawyer → Sequence 0: Black Emperor

The Black Emperor Pathway manipulates existing rules to serve its user, specializing in loophole exploitation, physicality, bribery, distortion, disorder, and domination. At high sequences it can exploit abstract laws, manipulate environments, magnify effects, isolate locations, and repeatedly resurrect while its order remains in place.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its authority is framed as the Shadow of Order, or a Distortion of Order, with recurring abilities centered on bribery, disorder, distortion, bestowment, magnify, exploit, majesty, and resurrection. The pathway's Above the Sequence is The Anarchy, and its corresponding Sefirah is the Nation of Disorder.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Earl of the Fallen: Exploit extends or ends states; Bestowment imposes negative effects, Magnify amplifies actions, and regional rules become manipulable.
  • Sequence 3 — Frenzied Mage: Superior materializes social authority as suppressive force; Frenzy randomly alters abilities, often benefiting the mage and harming opponents.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Duke of Entropy: Entropy progressively disorders abilities, thoughts, states, environments, or rules; Exploit reaches extraordinary distances and Distortion twists authorities' rules.
  • Sequence 1 — Prince of Abolition: Definition and Distortion rewrite existing Order, including what counts as substitutes, bribery, corruption, and other rules.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Black Emperor: Autocrat governs subordinate authorities through Order's loopholes; Distortion disrupts rules, and Resurrection returns the Black Emperor from mausoleums.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

The pathway’s earthly history is entangled with rival empires: Solomon became its Sequence 0, founded the Solomon Empire in the Fourth Epoch, and was killed in the rebellion led by Trunsoest and Alista Tudor; after a later resurrection, he rebuilt his empire. In the Fifth Epoch, Roselle—initially a Paragon Beyonder—also became Black Emperor. Much later, Loen’s George III pursued the same apotheosis through a hidden mausoleum-and-rule-changing ritual. The Twilight Hermit Order sheltered his scheme, while the Great Smog helped conceal its human cost

Story connections

  • The Fourth-Epoch succession: Solomon’s fall enabled Trunsoest and Alista Tudor to establish a joint empire; Solomon later returned and rebuilt his own
  • Roselle’s rise: Roselle changed pathways before becoming Black Emperor; he was later assassinated, then returned as an incomplete Black Emperor
  • George III’s failed bid: The ritual involved nine mausoleums, human trafficking, and political changes. The Great Smog obscured the plan; Klein and other allies ultimately interrupted George’s apotheosis
