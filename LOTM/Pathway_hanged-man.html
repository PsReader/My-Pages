Hanged Man Pathway

Sequence 9: Secrets Suppliant → Sequence 0: Hanged Man

The Hanged Man Pathway represents Degeneration, while also framing sacrifice and responsibility positively. Its Above the Sequence is God Almighty, and its corresponding Sefirah is the Chaos Sea.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its core motifs are Sin-Bearing and Sacrifice, with partial authorities over Shadow, Darkness, Degeneration, Filth, and Mutation. Signature capabilities include ritualistic magic, listening to secret entities, shadow manipulation, flesh-and-blood magic, soul-grazing, profane words, corruption, mutation, and degeneration.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Black Knight: Wields Cull of Spiritual Flesh, black armor, depraved-thought shadows, shadow commandeering, and externally released grazed souls.
  • Sequence 3 — Trinity Templar: Uses Shadow Substitute, three-part flesh-and-blood bodies, regeneration, and simultaneous release of three grazed souls.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Profane Presbyter: Weaponizes Profane Language to distort words, impose corruption or separation, curse targets, and deepen degeneration.
  • Sequence 1 — Dark Angel: Controls the Shadow World, corrodes and betrays targets, weaponizes profane speech, manifests darkness, and commands dependent souls.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Hanged Man: Embodies Sin-Bearing and Sacrifice while wielding partial authority over shadows, darkness, degeneration, filth, and mutation.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries and Circle of Inevitability. The Hanged Man pathway is framed not only by degeneration but by sin-bearing and sacrifice, giving its symbolism a narrative echo in the True Creator’s origin: after the Ancient Sun God’s fall, the betrayed god’s remnants—including the Hanged Man uniqueness and degeneration authority—contributed to the True Creator’s rebirth. The Aurora Order, whose primary pathway is Hanged Man, worships him; its plot to bring his spawn into the world drives the Tingen catastrophe, and it later participates in the Great Smog of Backlund. In the later Utopia crisis, Adam’s alliance with Zaratul puts Klein in Adam’s Corpse Cathedral

Story connections

  • Origin and betrayal: the Rose Redemption’s betrayal and the Ancient Sun God’s death precede the True Creator’s emergence from the remains; his rebirth is specifically tied to Hanged Man characteristics and degeneration authority
  • Aurora Order plots: the Order worships the True Creator and primarily uses the Hanged Man pathway; it is implicated in both the Tingen spawn incident and the Great Smog of Backlund
  • Utopia and Adam: Adam helps Zaratul enter Klein’s Utopia; after Klein is isolated from Sefirah Castle, he is taken to Adam’s Corpse Cathedral and learns more of Adam’s identity
  • Alger’s namesake and change: Alger is initially a Tyrant/Sailor-pathway Beyonder whose Tarot Club codename is the Hanged Man; by the end of Book Two he has switched to the actual Hanged Man pathway

Figures to know

  • Ancient Sun Godthe source from whose death and remains the True Creator emerged
  • True Creatorthe Hanged Man pathway’s Sequence 0 deity and Aurora Order’s object of worship
  • AdamAncient Sun God’s divinity-side figure; later fuses with the True Creator and intervenes in Klein’s Utopia advancement crisis
  • Alger WilsonTarot Club’s Hanged Man, later an actual Hanged Man-pathway angel after switching from Tyrant
