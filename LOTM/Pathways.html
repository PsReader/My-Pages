Fool Pathway

Sequence 9: Seer → Sequence 0: Fool

The Fool Pathway, also called the Seer Pathway, centers on fooling perceptions and reality. It specializes in divination, illusions, appearance-changing, bodily control, and turning targets into marionettes through Spirit Body Threads; at high sequences it develops into historical summoning, miracles, concealment, and conceptual grafting. Its Above the Sequence is the Lord of Mysteries, and its corresponding Sefirah is Sefirah Castle.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its main abilities span divination, illusion, shapeshifting, puppetry, concealment, History, Miracles, Grafting, and Fooling. The pathway article identifies its authority with Secrets and Changes, while the abilities article describes Fooling as partial authority over History, Time, Fate, Change, and Concealment.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Bizarro Sorcerer: Bestows Worms of Spirit to marionettes, conceals sequence level, greatly strengthens prior powers, and swaps positions with puppets.
  • Sequence 3 — Scholar of Yore: Uses History through past-self power borrowing, historical projections of people or objects, and concealment within the Historical Void.

Novel lore & story appearances

MAJOR SPOILERS: Zhou Mingrui’s luck ritual sends him into the body of the suicidal Klein Moretti, where he discovers the gray fog and Sefirah Castle. He becomes a Seer among the Nighthawks and advances through the Fool Pathway by combining acting, improvisation, and survival: Clown, Magician, Faceless, Marionettist, and the saint and angel stages. Above the fog, he creates the Tarot Club, posing as the ancient Mr. Fool while its members trade information, materials, and aid. After confronting Ince Zangwill, Zaratul, Antigonus, and Amon, Klein completes a history-fooling apotheosis as the Fool, then sleeps to resist the Celestial Worthy’s awakening

Story connections

  • Transmigration and the gray fog: The pre-Epoch luck ritual links Zhou Mingrui to Sefirah Castle, whose fog shelters divination, prayer, ritual exchanges, and hidden communications; the palace is later distinguished from the true Door of Light at the Castle’s core
  • Acting as survival and identity: Klein’s Seer-to-Fool climb rewards performing roles while preserving humanity. Sherlock Moriarty, Gehrman Sparrow/The World, and Mr. Fool are masks that let him investigate, gather resources, and manage the risks of godhood
  • Tarot Club as both tool and legend: It begins with Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson, then becomes a major hidden organization. Members know only Mr. Fool’s constructed persona, while the club’s history and missions gradually make that persona socially real
  • Apotheosis and aftermath: Klein uses Antigonus’s Fool resources and the apotheosis ritual to “fool” history, reaches Sequence 0, survives Amon’s assault on Sefirah Castle, and enters deep sleep while the Tarot Club carries forward the Fool’s name

Figures to know

  • Klein Moretti / Zhou MingruiTransmigrator, Fool Pathway Beyonder, Tarot Club convener, and eventual Fool
  • Audrey Hall and Alger WilsonThe first Tarot Club members pulled into the gray-fog gathering, later major allies
  • AntigonusHalf-Fool whose pathway resources and uniqueness become decisive to Klein’s ascent
  • AmonError-pathway rival who invades Sefirah Castle and forces Klein’s final gamble
  • Evernight GoddessA powerful supporter who helps Klein understand and manage the apotheosis crisis
