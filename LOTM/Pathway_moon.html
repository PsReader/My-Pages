Moon Pathway

Sequence 9: Apothecary → Sequence 0: Moon

The Moon Pathway specializes in healing, biological enhancement, healing and buffing potions, animal taming, regeneration, and darkness spells. At higher sequences it develops spirituality and Moon-domain control, long-distance summoning, life/creation manipulation, and Fertility and Proliferation authority; its Above the Sequence is the Goddess of Origin and its Sefirah is the Brood Hive.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

The pathway’s stated symbolism centers on spirituality, plants, beauty, parts of life, darkness, and mystery. Its main abilities include potioneering, spirituality, vampire physique, darkness magic, summoning, life manipulation, beauty, and Moonlight manipulation.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Shaman King: Interacts with nature for ritual magic, uses substitution spells, darkness and Moon spells, and Illusory Bats.
  • Sequence 3 — High Summoner: Opens a Door of Summoning to contract Spirit World creatures, with partial authority over summoning.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Life-Giver: Wields Life and partial Spirituality authority, healing directly, regrowing bodies, extending lifespans, and opening multiple summoning doors.
  • Sequence 1 — Beauty Goddess: Embodies Beauty, commands lower-sequence lifeforms, shapes favorable lunar environments, and dissolves into Crimson Light.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Moon: Commands Summoning, Beauty, Life, Spirituality, Darkness, Bizarreness, and subordinate control of plants, animals, humans, and creatures.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

The Moon pathway’s story is inseparable from the Sanguines: they call themselves Sanguine rather than “vampire,” and their worship of the Moon refers to ancestor Lilith, not the Primordial Moon. Spoilers: Lilith was believed to have fallen in the Second Epoch, but later survived by concealing herself as the Giant Queen Omebella, with help from the Evernight Goddess and Ancient Sun God. After Sanguine queen Auernia fell, an artifact carrying Lilith’s spiritual imprint was stolen by the Rose School of Thought and used to construct the public identity of the Primordial Moon. The school’s Primordial Moon faction began as a Moon-pathway branch of the Life School of Thought before defecting to the Rose School

Story connections

  • Lilith’s apparent death and return: Sanguine tradition remembers Lilith as the ancient ancestor, while the later revelation that she survived under Omebella’s identity reframes Earth Mother history. Lilith · Sanguines
  • An invented god-identity: The Rose School’s Primordial Moon faction was formed from defectors of the Life School of Thought; a stolen relic associated with Lilith and Auernia helped create the Primordial Moon’s public identity. Rose School of Thought · Lilith
  • Emlyn’s recruitment: Lilith’s apocalypse revelation reached Emlyn through Nibbs Odora, directing him toward the Fool; Emlyn subsequently joined the Tarot Club. Lilith · Nibbs Odora · Emlyn White

Figures to know

  • LilithSanguine Ancestor and former Moon deity; later revealed as the Earth Mother who survived by taking Omebella’s identity. Lilith
  • AuerniaSanguine queen and wife of the Night Emperor; her fall is tied to the relic later used to stage the Primordial Moon identity. Sanguines · Lilith
  • Emlyn WhiteSanguine and Moon-pathway Beyonder whose recruitment connects Sanguine politics to the Tarot Club. Emlyn White
  • Nibbs OdoraSanguine elder who delivered Lilith’s revelation to Emlyn and set his connection to the Fool in motion. Nibbs Odora · Lilith
