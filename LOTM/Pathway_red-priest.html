Red Priest Pathway

Sequence 9: Hunter → Sequence 0: Red Priest

The Red Priest Pathway centers on war-related abilities: fire, traps, enhanced physical ability, weakness-finding, conspiracy, and provocation. At high Sequences it expands into weather control, steel-like flame manipulation, army coordination, conquest, and the authorities of War and Destruction; its Above the Sequence is Calamity of Destruction and its Sefirah is the City of Calamity.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its progression moves from hunting, survival, traps, and provocation into pyrokinesis, conspiracy, precision attacks against weaknesses, weaponizing, command-and-control, weather manipulation, conquest, and large-scale destruction. The pathway also has a masculine symbolism tied to the Original Creator and shares a high-sequence group with the Demoness Pathway.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Iron-blooded Knight: Weaponizes ordinary objects, links and shares with troops, concentrates or spreads damage, and galvanizes body and flames into steel.
  • Sequence 3 — War Bishop: Boosts allies and weakens enemies through war songs, creates Fog of War, gathers army power, and invokes sacrifices.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Weather Warlock: Controls regional weather, gathers strength across a team, weaponizes materials, and travels as fast flame or meteor.
  • Sequence 1 — Conqueror: Subjugates targets, turns objects into soldiers, shares rank and abilities, gathers massed power, and commands vast temporary teams.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Red Priest: Commands War, Conquest, Destruction, Sacrifices, and planetary-scale team power-sharing, including calamity, flame, and weakness-locking authorities.

Novel lore & story appearances

The Red Priest pathway is framed as War and Destruction, and its story history becomes a dynastic power struggle as much as a supernatural one. Medici, an innate mythical creature and one of the Ancient Sun God’s eight Kings of Angels, accommodated the pathway’s Uniqueness and led the Red of War Army; Alista Tudor later killed Medici, Sauron, and Einhorn to become Red Priest, fracturing the Tudor–Trunsoest United Empire. In Circle of Inevitability, the Red Priest-dominated Iron and Blood Cross Order seeks the Blood Emperor’s legacy, while Medici manipulates it to find Vermonda Sauron in Fourth Epoch Trier

Story connections

  • Fourth Epoch succession and empire collapse: Alista’s alliance with Adam and Amon traps the three apex Red Priest angels; his ascension to Red Priest coincides with the breakup of the Tudor–Trunsoest United Empire
  • The Red Angel’s return: Medici’s death leaves his spirit fused with those of Sauron and Einhorn. As the Red Angel Evil Spirit, he later possesses Ince Zangwill and pursues his own agenda in Book One
  • Trier legacy plot: In Book Two, the Iron and Blood Cross Order pursues the Blood Emperor’s legacy in Fourth Epoch Trier. Medici manipulates the Order’s members to locate Vermonda Sauron, and Lumian becomes central to the Order’s “Hostel plan.”
