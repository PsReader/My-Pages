Xio Derecha

Justiciar · Sequence 4: Imperative Mage (Justiciar Pathway)

Xio Derecha is a Beyonder from the Arbiter. She is a friend of Fors Wall. She is currently a member of the Tarot Club. Her codename is Judgment.
