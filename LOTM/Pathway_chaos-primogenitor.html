Chaos Primogenitor Pathway

Outer Deity: Mother Goddess of Depravity · Boon advancement

The Chaos Primogenitor is a non-standard pathway associated with supernatural intimidation, floral and alchemical knowledge, blood manipulation, heretical magic, and the creation of new life. At high levels it holds authority over Reproduction, Propagation, and Reality, and its Earth-side advancement is obtained through Boons from the Mother Goddess of Depravity rather than ordinary potion consumption.

Non-standard pathway lore and Outer Deity corruption details contain major story spoilers.

Detailed description

The Chaos Primogenitor is a non-standard pathway associated with supernatural intimidation, floral and alchemical knowledge, blood manipulation, heretical magic, and the creation of new life. At high levels it holds authority over Reproduction, Propagation, and Reality, and its Earth-side advancement is obtained through Boons from the Mother Goddess of Depravity rather than ordinary potion consumption.

Paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Represents Evil and Corruption, including the exploitation of life and the distortion of living systems.
  • Can impose a continuous weakening effect on targets within reality and fix mystical connections, spirits, or consciousness in place.
  • Uses Reproduction and Propagation to nurture, create, or transform life, including the catastrophic birth-symbolism associated with the Chaos Primogenitor.
  • Can manipulate blood, perform heretical magic, and combine floral or alchemical forces into corruptive biological effects.

Corruption from Mother Goddess of Depravity

The Mother Goddess of Depravity is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Mother Goddess of Depravity. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
