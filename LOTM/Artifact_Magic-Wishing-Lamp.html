Magic Wishing Lamp

Grade 0 · Code 0-05

Overview

Magic Wishing Lamp (0-05) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: A golden lamp covered with mysterious and complicated symbols

Function

Able to grant up to 10 wishes of its holder.

Downside

The granted wish would always come in a distorted and horrible manner and accompanied with terrible consequences. The holder can avoid the harm brought by the first two wishes, through proper wording and preparations, but the third wish will have terrible consequences.

Status

Damaged by Chaos Sea
