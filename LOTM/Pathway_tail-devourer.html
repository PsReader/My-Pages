Tail-Devourer Pathway

Outer Deity: Primordial Hunger · Boon advancement

The Tail-Devourer is a non-standard pathway centered on analyzing and processing ingredients into dishes, drinks, and concoctions. It enhances digestion, deprives others of Beyonder powers, and digests or absorbs Beyonder characteristics and mystical items. Its high authorities are Devouring, Convergence, and Hunger, and Earth-side advancement comes through Boons from Primordial Hunger.

Non-standard pathway lore and Outer Deity corruption details contain major story spoilers.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Forcibly converges targets and can pull true bodies or symbolic influences toward the Outer Deity.
  • Devours, digests, utilizes, and erases objects, powers, Avatars, or Marionettes so completely that nothing may remain recoverable.
  • Can consume hostile mental corruption and ravings, turning contamination itself into something that can be processed.
  • Its hunger can spread catastrophically: devouring powerful pathways or entities may trigger universal convergence, instability, and the loss of life-supporting conditions.

Corruption from Primordial Hunger

The Primordial Hunger is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Known corruptive effects

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Primordial Hunger. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
