Inexhaustible Poison

Grade 2 · Code 2-030

Overview

Inexhaustible Poison (2-030) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 2 Sealed Artifact. Grade 2 artifacts cover the middle Sequences, roughly Sequence 5 to Sequence 7.

It is described as: A silver cup filled with a crystal-clear liquid.

Function

Tempts and kills the drinker.

Downside

Affects everyone around it including the user.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.
