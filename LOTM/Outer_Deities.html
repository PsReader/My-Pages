Outer Deities & Corruption

The entities beyond the standard pathways—and the ways their Boons, symbols, and authorities corrupt the world.

Outer Deities are not simply distant gods. Their pathways can enter the world through Boons, prayer, contaminated knowledge, symbolic influence, and the gradual replacement of natural laws with an alien authority. The profiles below summarize the main corruptive patterns associated with the ten non-standard pathways in this archive.

Mother Goddess of Depravity

Chaos Primogenitor Pathway

  • Represents Evil and Corruption, including the exploitation of life and the distortion of living systems.
  • Can impose a continuous weakening effect on targets within reality and fix mystical connections, spirits, or consciousness in place.
  • Uses Reproduction and Propagation to nurture, create, or transform life, including the catastrophic birth-symbolism associated with the Chaos Primogenitor.
  • Can manipulate blood, perform heretical magic, and combine floral or alchemical forces into corruptive biological effects.
Read the Chaos Primogenitor guide →

Mother Tree of Desire

Patriarch Pathway

  • Manipulates desires in the hearts of creatures and can induce twisted impulses in other people.
  • Awakens survival instincts across a region, even affecting powerful beings and destabilizing their judgment.
  • Spreads corruption through dreams, acting, environmental influence, and the mutation of living bodies.
  • Its Outer Deity corrupted the Chained God and the Dark Side of the Universe, turning desire into an engine of indiscriminate violence, sacrifice, and biological transformation.
Read the Patriarch guide →

Son of Chaos

Chaos Mist Pathway

  • Holds authority over Blasphemy, Truth, Chaos, contracts, transactions, and uncertain outcomes.
  • Can make supernatural contracts that bind even powerful entities, with catastrophic consequences for breaking them.
  • Uses Boons to grant advancement or even godhood, allowing the Outer Deity to create agents without standard potion progression.
  • Knowledge of Outer Deities can itself become a vector of contamination, secret observation, and corruption.
Read the Chaos Mist guide →

Primordial Hunger

Tail-Devourer Pathway

  • Forcibly converges targets and can pull true bodies or symbolic influences toward the Outer Deity.
  • Devours, digests, utilizes, and erases objects, powers, Avatars, or Marionettes so completely that nothing may remain recoverable.
  • Can consume hostile mental corruption and ravings, turning contamination itself into something that can be processed.
  • Its hunger can spread catastrophically: devouring powerful pathways or entities may trigger universal convergence, instability, and the loss of life-supporting conditions.
Read the Tail-Devourer guide →

Circle of Inevitability

Eternal Aeon Pathway

  • Anchors a chosen tributary of the River of Fate so that interference with a designated project becomes increasingly doomed to fail.
  • Uses Cycle and Loop to reset regions, undo developments, and force events or battles to repeat.
  • Fixes a person’s fate onto madness or suffering, preserving the body while turning the future into a predetermined catastrophe.
  • Uses Substitution to replace figures in the River of Fate, switch bodies or identities, and create profound mystical links for pursuit.
Read the Eternal Aeon guide →

Supernova Dominator

Condenser Pathway

  • Creates intense gravitational fields that bend or shatter matter and can reach intangible targets such as Fate, thoughts, and Spirit Bodies.
  • Summons or controls stars and supernovae, producing destructive eruptions capable of overwhelming layers of defense and many Avatars.
  • Forms black holes that compress matter, capture light and heat, slow time, and crush entire stellar dominions.
  • Controls fusion and fundamental forces to create matter, reverse black holes into white holes, and release blinding or annihilating light.
Read the Condenser guide →

Inextinguishable Ravings

Everlasting Pathway

  • Awakens the Original Creator’s spiritual imprint within Sefirot, Uniquenesses, and Beyonder characteristics, leaving exposed beings vulnerable to madness.
  • Projects a voice directly into protected spiritual structures, bypassing Marionettes, Avatars, and Afterimages to implant intent.
  • Manifests as frenzied thoughts or formless consciousness, producing direct mental corruption and disordered spirits.
  • Injury to the deity can spread chaotic consciousness across a region or star domain, while philosophical revelations inflict physical and mental anguish on listeners.
Read the Everlasting guide →

Monarch of Decay

Second Law Pathway

  • Causes planets, ecosystems, and living beings to decay, rot to death, or transform into loyal and disgusting creatures.
  • Destabilizes the internal balance of powerful beings and makes failure, collapse, and corruption increasingly likely.
  • Drags a region’s time toward its conclusion, slowing surrounding events while hastening the approach of an endpoint.
  • Uses Certain Death and Sure Hit symbolism to make fatal attacks or unavoidable decay into supernatural certainties.
Read the Second Law guide →

High-Dimensional Overseer

Sublunary Eye Pathway

  • Freely traverses higher and lower dimensions and changes the dimensionality of other beings, countering effects such as parasitism, puppetry, and teleportation.
  • Reduces targets into paper-like or painted forms that merge with scenery and lose ordinary freedom of movement.
  • Creates painting-worlds with their own suns, planets, rules, and principles, then traps powerful enemies inside them.
  • Within its paintings the Overseer can govern reality with near-total omnipotence and create painted entities capable of True God-level destruction.
Read the Sublunary Eye guide →

Goddess of Fate

Eternal Edict Pathway

  • Weaves new fates and possibilities for herself or others, destabilizing a powerful being’s body by altering its destiny.
  • Severs a target’s River of Fate and tributaries, indirectly controlling its future, dreams, and available outcomes.
  • Applies Final Judgement so that beings below the Above-the-Sequence level helplessly face an approaching end.
  • Returns consciousness and spirit from the River of Fate to manifest a new body, and can weave her own fate to evade otherwise overwhelming cosmic forces.
