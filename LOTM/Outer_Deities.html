Outer Deities & Corruption

The entities beyond the standard pathways—and the ways their Boons, symbols, and authorities corrupt the world.

Outer Deities are entities from outside the Earth barrier who corrupt the world through Boons, prayer, contaminated knowledge, symbolic influence, and replacement of natural laws. Many established organizations worshipping them operate in secrecy.

Mother Goddess of Depravity

Chaos Primogenitor Pathway

  • Represents Evil and Corruption, including exploitation of life and distortion of living systems.
  • Can impose weakening effects and fix mystical connections, spirits, or consciousness in place.
  • Authority over Reproduction and Propagation; can create/transmute life in corruptive ways.
  • Manipulates blood and performs heretical magic combining floral/alchemical forces.

Related Organizations: Holy Mother Order, Mother Five-Grains, Sole Spirit Society, The Great Mother, Nightstalkers, Tree God Sect, School of God's Descent, School of Existence

Mother Tree of Desire

Patriarch Pathway

  • Focuses on reproduction, indulgence, and binding through desire.
