Sun Pathway

Sequence 9: Bard → Sequence 0: Sun

The Sun Pathway combines priestly light and holy magic with self- and ally-enhancement, purification, exorcism, and hard-to-break contracts. At high sequences it develops Justice and Order, broad Light authority, and the capacity to create a holy domain or function as a Sun.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its central authorities are Light, Holiness, and Order, with Sun, Faith, and Contracts as related lesser authorities. Common expressions include bardic enhancement, fire and light spells, notarization, purification, exorcism, justice-based empowerment, holy domains, and inextinguishable light.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Unshadowed: Direct Purification removes degeneration, corruption, darkness, evil, ailments, and unclean influences, even from Beyonders or characteristics.
  • Sequence 3 — Justice Mentor: Defines personal Justice and Order, empowering aligned actions, weakening violations, enforcing Holy Contracts, and strengthening purification domains.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Lightseeker: Gains Sun and Light authority, becoming a miniature Sun, moving as pure light, resisting corruption, and wielding Spear of Light.
  • Sequence 1 — White Angel: Commands partial Holiness, Order, Faith, and Sun authority, creating Holy Kingdoms, fervor, pure white light, and solar radiance.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Sun: Embodies Inextinguishable Light and authorities over Light, Order, Contracts, and Holiness; wherever light exists, it can illuminate and destroy.

Sequence highlights are paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers: The Sun pathway is woven into the Ancient Sun God’s legacy: Aucuses, once his White Angel, joined the Rose Redemption faction before becoming the Eternal Blazing Sun after the Ancient Sun God’s fall; the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun, founded in the Third Epoch, controls the pathway. In the Forsaken Land, the City of Silver kept faith in the Ancient Sun God as Creator through a 2,582-year darkness and a curse that left kin to kill their dying relatives. Derrick Berg sought the Sun pathway to free his people; later expeditions lead him and Colin Iliad to Sasrir and a fatal confrontation

Story connections

  • Major spoilers: Derrick’s desire to “become the sun” is personal as well as communal: the City of Silver’s curse forced him to kill his parents, and he sought the Sun pathway to free his people
  • Major spoilers: In the True Creator’s temple expedition, Derrick helps expose the team’s repeated time loop; his observations and guidance from the Tarot Club help Colin’s team break it
  • Major spoilers: At the Giant King’s Court, Colin Iliad and Derrick confront the Dark Angel evil spirit Sasrir. Colin dies restraining Sasrir and asks Derrick to open the gate toward the sunlight beyond

Figures to know

  • Derrick BergCity of Silver member and Sun-pathway Beyonder whose hope of freeing his people shapes his Tarot Club role
