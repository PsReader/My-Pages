Pride Armor

Grade 2 · Code 2-247

Overview

Pride Armor (2-247) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 2 Sealed Artifact. Grade 2 artifacts cover the middle Sequences, roughly Sequence 5 to Sequence 7.

It is described as: A silver-white full-body armor.

Function

Grants the user abilities of a Dawn Paladin

Downside

  • Wearer hold disdain for those standing behind them and targets concealed in the darkness or invisible. They may attack them.
  • It indiscriminately eliminates weak humans within a 50-meter radius. Should the wearer weaken, they too become a target.
  • The wearer of the armor eventually experiences varying degrees of betrayal, irrespective of whether they still wear the item.
  • The wearer is vulnerable to its attacks when using other mystical items.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
