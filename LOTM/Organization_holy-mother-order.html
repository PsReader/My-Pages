Background

The Holy Mother Order is a secret organization that worships the Mother Goddess of Depravity, an Outer Deity associated with the Chaos Primogenitor Pathway.

Among the Outer Deities, only the Mother Goddess of Depravity and the Mother Tree of Desire were able to establish unified world-spanning factions within the Earth's barrier. Other Outer Deities established organizations of varying sizes in different regions; the Holy Mother Order is one such organization associated with the Mother Goddess of Depravity.
