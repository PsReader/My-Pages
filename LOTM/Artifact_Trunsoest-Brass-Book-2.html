Trunsoest Brass Book

Uniqueness · No code recorded

Overview

Trunsoest Brass Book is recorded in the archive as a Uniqueness Sealed Artifact. Uniquenesses are the singular Beyonder characteristics of a Sequence 0 true god. They are not ordinary sealed artifacts, and the archive lists them alongside the grades for completeness.

It is described as: A book bound by thin brass sheets.

Function

Introducing laws, offenders of the law will automatically be punished accordingly or the action itself will become impossible to do.

Downside

It cumulatively writes new laws, eventually creating a system wherein one would be punished regardless of what they do, that includes the user himself, unless he could negotiate immunity with it.

Status

Accommodated

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
