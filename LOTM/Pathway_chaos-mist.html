Overview

The Chaos Mist Pathway is the gray between white and black, the fog between light and darkness, the bridge between order ( Justiciar) and shadows ( Black Emperor).[1] They represent Grayness that is the ultimate Truth of the universe, and are the manifestation of Chaos that renders all that is certain to become no longer certain. Beyonders of this Pathway can act as the "platform" that enables transactions between different parties, forming a Vortex consisting of many different forces.[2]

This Pathway is one of the new Pathways introduced in Circle of Inevitability. It exists outside the 22 Standard Pathways, but is still classified into Sequence 9 to Sequence 0.[3]

On Earth, the Beyonders of this Pathway do not advance through Potions, but by praying to the Outer Deity Uncertain Mist, who is the Above the Sequence of this Pathway, for a Boon. The corresponding Sefirah is the remaining part of the Son of Chaos's Sefirah that was not attracted to Earth and became the Nation of Disorder.

This Pathway is the only Pathway under the Uncertain Mist.[4]

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Holds authority over Blasphemy, Truth, Chaos, contracts, transactions, and uncertain outcomes.
  • Can make supernatural contracts that bind even powerful entities, with catastrophic consequences for breaking them.
  • Uses Boons to grant advancement or even godhood, allowing the Outer Deity to create agents without standard potion progression.
  • Knowledge of Outer Deities can itself become a vector of contamination, secret observation, and corruption.

Corruption from Son of Chaos

The Son of Chaos is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Son of Chaos. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
