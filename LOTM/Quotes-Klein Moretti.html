Klein Moretti Quotes

Twelve lines from Klein Moretti, with the chapter each one comes from.

"Free things cost the most."

"The oldest and strongest emotion of mankind is fear, and the oldest and strongest fear is the fear of the unknown."

Klein Moretti, Chapter 9 (originally H.P. Lovecraft, Supernatural Horror in Literature)

"A true professor can communicate with people gently and politely, but he can also educate the other person using the principles of physics by raising a cane to convince someone when there is an obstacle in communication."

Klein to Melissa, Chapter 98

"Fate never repeats itself indefinitely. It always brings us some surprises."

Klein Moretti, Chapter 153

"Knowledge can change one's destiny, and diligence will result in Glory."

Klein to Melissa, Chapter 183

"Life is full of hard choices..."

Klein Moretti, Chapter 401

"As long as you choose this path, you'll constantly be accompanied by threats and madness. You can beat them a hundred times, a thousand times, but as long as you lose once, you'll end up like that fallen bishop."

Klein Moretti, Chapter 513

"A price is always exacted for what fate bestows, isn't it?"

"Even if it's meaningless, some things still ought to be done."

"In this world, nothing can be easily resolved without taking risks. Sometimes, I do something with the thought that 'death is a possibility.'"

"Miracles are only for a moment, but fate is often a long-lasting event."

Klein to Arrodes, Chapter 1314

"I won't lose too much, just myself. There are always some things that are more important than others."

The Fool, Chapter 1386
