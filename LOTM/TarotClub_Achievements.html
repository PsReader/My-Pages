Tarot Club - Achievements

1. Book One: Lord of Mysteries

The achievements of the Tarot Club across Lord of the Mysteries, from the founding gathering to the end of the story.

Qilangos

The Hanged Man gave the offer to kill the Tyrant Pathway Beyonder Rear Admiral Qilangos in a Tarot meeting. Justice accepted the offer and hired Fors and Xio to investigate him. Later, Justice found him during a ball and prayed to The Fool. Eventually, Azik killed him, while Justice and The Hanged Man assumed it was done by one of Mr. Fool's followers.

Steel Maveti

The Hanged Man realized that The World was in Bayam, and offered to help. The World then invited him to the fight against Steel Maveti. This was the first time The Hanged Man and The World met in reality. The Hanged Man informed his Church and lured Maveti out, while The World and Danitz conducted the fight and killed him. They split up the bounty.

Sea of Ruins

The World asked The Hermit for the opportunity to board onto The Future to search for mermaids. They went through a series of adventures along with all the crews and The World advanced to Sequence 5: Marionettist.

Primitive Island

The Hanged Man gave The World the coordinates of an island he has once explored with Qilangos as the price of the Ocean Songster characteristic. Later, they explored the island together and discovered the painting showing the great betrayal of the three angels: White, Wind and Wisdom. The World 'sacrificed' the Tyrant Card to Mr. Fool.

Cielf June

The Hanged Man found the artisan Cielf June working with believers of Primordial Moon. He then invited The Hermit to capture Cielf and made him return what was The Hanged Man's and had him hand in all his artifacts. The Hermit then brought Cielf onto The Future to ensure observation and benefits.

Ernes Boyar

This was the first mission that involved most Tarot Club members. The Moon decided to get revenge against Viscount Ernes Boyar for giving him wrong information about the Abandoned Castle, which almost killed Judgement and The Magician. Judgement investigated and followed Ernes; The Star dragged Ernes into a dream; Justice disguised herself as a paperboy and hypnotized Ernes; The Moon was the bait. The Hanged Man gave his advice that they needed to mislead the Sanguines in some ways. Mr. Fool monitored the whole action above the Gray Fog.

Amon Avatars

Amon's avatar found Klein in his house, and The Star (with Pallez) came to fight as planned. After defeating Amon, The World brought Justice to Hazel's house to clear her memories about Amon and hypnotized everyone who had been parasitized by Amon to pray to Evernight Goddess afterward.

Groselle's Travels

The World invited The Star and Justice to explore the subconscious sea of Groselle's Travels. They discovered a non-floating city that exactly resembled the City of Miracle, Liveseyd. At the end they encountered a dangerous corruption locked away by the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt.

Botis

The World hired Tarot Club members to hunt Botis in order to obtain his Worms of Star (Worms of Cosmos). The Hermit and Justice hunted Botis together, while Judgement hid aside and interrupted Botis with her Justiciar pathway abilities, The Magician was only a bait and was captured by the Box of the Great Old Ones at the start of the operation. Mr. Fool, as always, monitored the action above the Gray Fog. After the operation The Star 'named' the Tarot Club for the first time. The spoils of war from Botis were divided among the participants.

Shaman King Klarman

The Moon, along with Rose School of Thought Temperance faction members (Sharron, Maric, and Reinette Tinekerr) and The World planned together to hunt down a member of the Rose School of Thought Indulgence faction from the Moon Pathway. Their choice was Shaman King Klarman. The Moon partnered with Sharron to fight, with the help of a Grade 1 Sealed Artifact of the Sun Pathway, while The World and Reinette acted as bait to divert the attention of Suah. In the end, they managed to kill Klarman.

Giant King's Court

Klein was forced by the True Creator to meet Sasrir. Together with the Sun, Colin Iliad and Lovia, they defeated Sasir and opened the door to the outside world at the cost of the latter two's lives.

2. Book Two: Circle of Inevitability

The achievements of the Tarot Club during Circle of Inevitability.

Tree of Shadow

The Seven of Wands, the Two of Cups, Judgement, and Magician fought and prevented the emergence of the Tree of Shadow, which was planted by the Mother Tree of Desire long ago in Trier.

I Know Someone

Magician hired Will Auceptin in order to give Jenna enough luck to locate I Know Someone in an asylum in Trier. The Seven of Wands and the Two of Cups fought against him, and with the help of Justice and Magician, they survived and killed him.
