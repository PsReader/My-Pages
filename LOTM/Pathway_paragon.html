Paragon Pathway

Sequence 9: Savant → Sequence 0: Paragon

The Paragon Pathway specializes in natural-world knowledge and item-crafting, combining scientific, historical, and mechanical expertise with the creation and safe use of powerful Beyonder weapons and sealed items. At high sequences, it can animate creations, disintegrate structures, extract environmental information, alter physical principles, perceive essences, and advance civilization through technology.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Overview

Pathway themes & abilities

Its core themes are knowledge, memory, craftsmanship, appraisal, ritualistic magic, manufacturing, artificial life, technology, disintegration, physics/principles, and essence analysis. The pathway emphasizes scientific learning and reasoning without the high-spirituality dangers or disaster attraction associated with some other pathways; its high-end authority centers on reality-leaning information, civilization, education, learning, manufacturing, and analysis.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Alchemist: Creates soul-infused life and temporary dolls or objects; enhanced manufacturing supports mechanical bodies and devices.
  • Sequence 3 — Arcane Scholar: Transforms into knowledge, extracts environmental information, manipulates surroundings, absorbs life force, and disintegrates structures.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Knowledge Magister: Creates imagined wonders, alters physical principles, analyzes essences, teaches or tests others, and learns instantly.
  • Sequence 1 — Illuminator: Wields Civilization and Education authorities to restore structures, influence targets, create cultural effects, and enlighten.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Paragon: Holds reality-leaning authority over discovered knowledge, civilization, physical principles, education, learning, manufacturing, and analysis.

Novel lore & story appearances

Roselle Gustav began as a Savant (the Paragon pathway’s earlier name) after the Church of Craftsmanship gave him two pathway options, and he chose Savant; his inventions, including the steam engine and improved sailboats, helped launch Intis’s industrial revolution. The church’s god then ordered the church’s renaming to God of Steam and Machinery, linking Paragon’s technological role to a major institution. Their relationship later soured: Roselle suspected the god sought to control him through his son Bornova, and the church joined Roselle’s eventual assassination. Much later, Bernadette tried to save her corrupted father; during Project Vortex, Roselle intervened against the Uncertain Mist and perished, with resurrection deferred for a century

Story connections

  • A pathway reshapes a church: Roselle’s Savant inventions fueled Intis’s industrial turn, and the deity’s name changed from God of Craftsmanship to God of Steam and Machinery
  • Innovation turns into political and religious conflict: Roselle suspected the Steam god was using Bornova to control him; later, the church joined the forces that assassinated Roselle
  • Father and daughter amid cosmic danger (major spoiler): Bernadette searched for a way to save Roselle. In Project Vortex, he acted against the Uncertain Mist, died in the process, and was set to resurrect after a century

Figures to know

  • Roselle GustavParagon/Savant Beyonder, inventor and ruler of Intis; his industrial reforms, pathway switch, assassination plan, and later sacrifice connect these events
  • God of Steam and Machinerythe deity whose church controls the Paragon pathway and whose name and public standing changed after Roselle’s industrial revolution
  • Bernadette GustavRoselle’s daughter; she pursued answers about his fate and participated in the attempt to address his corruption
  • Bornova GustavRoselle’s son, whom Roselle suspected the Steam god was using to influence him
