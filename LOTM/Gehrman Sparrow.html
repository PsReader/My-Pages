Gehrman Sparrow

Klein Moretti’s adventurer identity · The World

Gehrman Sparrow is an identity assumed by Klein Moretti after he leaves Backlund. Under this name, Klein presents himself as a cold, dangerous adventurer and pirate bounty hunter. “The World” is his Tarot Club title.

This page contains major spoilers for Lord of Mysteries.

Purpose of the identity

Klein uses the Gehrman Sparrow identity while traveling through the seas and seeking mermaids for his advancement ritual. Maintaining the role also helps him digest the Faceless potion. The identity becomes widely known through his work as an adventurer and bounty hunter.

People associated with Gehrman

  • Blazing Danitz, his companion during part of the sea journey.
  • Reinette Tinekerr, his messenger.
  • Klein Moretti, the person behind the identity.

For the full character record, see Klein Moretti’s profile. For his Tarot Club role, see Tarot Club Members.

Story role

Gehrman is one of several public identities Klein uses to move through the world. Compared with his earlier detective identity, Gehrman is deliberately severe and confrontational. The performance supports Klein’s work at sea while concealing his connection to the Tarot Club’s mysterious leader.

As “The World,” Klein speaks to the other Tarot Club members through this persona. The identity is referenced again in Circle of Inevitability.
