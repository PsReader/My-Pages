Error Pathway

Sequence 9: Marauder → Sequence 0: Error

The Error Pathway governs deception and theft, progressing from stealing physical objects to abilities, thoughts, life, fate, identity, time, and authorities. At high Sequences, its Beyonders use parasitism, avatars, fate manipulation, time manipulation, and rule loopholes to behave like an Error or bug.

Overview

The Error Pathway governs deception and theft, progressing from stealing physical objects to abilities, thoughts, life, fate, identity, time, and authorities. At high Sequences, its Beyonders use parasitism, avatars, fate manipulation, time manipulation, and rule loopholes to behave like an Error or bug.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its core skill is Theft, whose scope becomes increasingly conceptual as the pathway advances; related capabilities include deceit, decryption, parasitism, avatars, fate manipulation, and time control. Its symbolism centers on parasitism, time, and fate, while the highest Sequence embodies loopholes and errors in rules.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Parasite: Uses Worms of Time for parasitism, hosts, and avatars; steals abilities, life aspects, position, and distance.
  • Sequence 3 — Mentor of Deceit: Extends deceit to abstract concepts and reality’s rules, manipulates mystical connections, discerns weaknesses, and steals three powers at once.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Trojan Horse of Destiny: Exploits loopholes, senses and alters fate, steals identity, self-awareness, characteristics, connections, and up to six powers.
  • Sequence 1 — Worm of Time: Temporarily steals time, anchors, and authorities; manipulates time, ages targets, reverses time briefly, and reads recent history.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Error: Embodies errors and loopholes, deceives rules, creates bugs, steals authorities, and redirects or avoids attacks through substitutions.

Novel lore & story appearances

Error is one of the three pathways grouped beneath Lord of Mysteries (with Fool and Door), and its central symbolism expands from theft into destiny, time, and loopholes in rules. Amon, a child of the Ancient Sun God and a Third Epoch King of Angels, became Sequence 0 Error by replacing Mr. Door in the apotheosis ritual; after Klein defeated him, Amon revived through an avatar and departed for the cosmos. In Volume 6, Amon captures Klein and attempts to entice him into surrendering his destiny. Pallez Zoroast's history then shows Amon's conflict reaching Leonard's circle: a trap tied to the Jacob Family treasure costs Pallez his Sequence 1 status

Story connections

  • Klein vs. Amon: Amon holds Klein captive during the journey into the Forsaken Land of the Gods and tries to win Klein's destiny; the volume summary records Klein's escape with the Evernight Goddess's help
  • Amon vs. Pallez: Pallez's attempt to recover using the Jacob Family's secret treasure turns out to be Amon's trap, and Pallez loses his Sequence 1 characteristic
  • Error's pathway succession: Amon replaces Mr. Door in the apotheosis ritual to become Sequence 0 Error, then accommodates the Door Uniqueness; these events bind the Error and Door lineages in the story
