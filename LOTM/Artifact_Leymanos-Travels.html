Leymano's Travels

Grade 2 · No code recorded

Overview

Leymano's Travels is recorded in the archive as a Grade 2 Sealed Artifact. Grade 2 artifacts cover the middle Sequences, roughly Sequence 5 to Sequence 7.

It is described as: A palm-sized notebook with a hard copper-green outer casing.

Function

Copies Beyonder abilities it has seen, allowing them to be reused only once.

Downside

Makes the user lost. This can be avoided by smearing his blood on its cover.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
