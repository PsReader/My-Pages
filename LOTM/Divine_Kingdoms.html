Divine Kingdoms

神国 · Shén guó · Summoned realms that embody a True God's power and concept

Definition

Divine Kingdoms (神国, Shén guó) are summoned areas that embody the power and concept of the True God that created it. Within their Divine Kingdoms, their strength increases and their enemies are suppressed.

Some Angels are capable of creating their own version of Divine Kingdoms, however, their Divine Kingdoms are incomplete. Only a Sequence 0 True God can create a complete Divine Kingdom. The size of a True God's Divine Kingdom can cover a star.1 Sefirot like the Sefirah Castle come with their own inherent Divine Kingdom.2

Types of Divine Kingdoms

Divine Kingdoms are divided into two categories.3

  1. Rules-based Divine Kingdoms: they could be forcibly created, however once the Divine Kingdom's rules are fixed, its owner would similarly be restricted to a certain extent. They have to bear the corresponding responsibility for the benefits obtained.4 These include but are not limited to the following:
    • Realm of Mysteries, the Fool's complete Divine Kingdom.
      • Within it, every door was Reassembled with different items. If one forcefully broke through them, they would trigger unknown effects, making it hard for others to break through with brute force.4
      • However, the corresponding Deity will definitely be in one of the rooms, not somewhere else.
    • The Error's Divine Kingdom, an Erroneous environment.35
      • It can be constructed to allow creating Errors while not directly affecting the target.
    • The Door's Divine Kingdom, a self-defined Alternate World.36
    • The Visionary's Divine Kingdom, unique according to their own imagination and thus possesses a variety of different purposes and abilities.7
  2. Direct Divine Kingdoms: involving ruling a certain region or realm, then turning it into one's own Divine Kingdom. These include but are not limited to the following:
    • The Red Priest's Divine Kingdom, his Divine Kingdom is formed through completely Conquering a land, battlefield, or special area. This is a deeper conceptualization of Conquest.
    • The Demoness's Divine Kingdom, her Divine Kingdom is tied to the Mirror World.
    • The Hanged Man's Divine Kingdom, the entire Shadow World, or something similar to the Shadow World that has been created by them.8
    • The Darkness's Divine Kingdom, they can turn Concealed Worlds into their Divine Kingdom.9
