1. List of Members

"A Major Arcana card symbolizes not just you personally, but also the forces you represent."

- Fors Wall to Lumian Lee on Circle of Inevitability, Chapter 1034

2. Book Two Additions (1359-1360)

Book Two: Circle of Inevitability adds three Major Arcana to the organization. The Chariot and Temperance join during The Fool's dream, and The Empress joins after the Apocalypse.

The Chariot became a Great Old One during the Apocalypse and left the organization, so The Empress joined in his place. From 1360 on, the remaining Major Arcana members are Angels.

3. Membership System

Official members, invited by Mr. Fool, belong to the Major Arcana. Peripheral members, invited by official ones, belong to the Minor Arcana.

By 1358, Franca believed there to be at most 23 Minor Arcana in the world, based on how many different cards have been left behind at reported Beyonder incidents. There might be fewer, because of imitators. The Two of Cups, the Four of Swords, and three other Minor Arcana were active in Trier.

4. Notes

  • The join date refers to the date when the member officially picks his or her Tarot Card alias.
  • The sequence refers to the current sequence of the member.
  • The World was believed to be an Angel under Mr. Fool before learning his true identity.
  • Klein considers Will Auceptin, Pallez Zoroast, Azik Eggers, and Reinette Tinekerr as auxiliary members.
  • Klein's requirements for a new member: ambitious, ethical, cultured, disciplined.

5. Trivia

Several early members of the Tarot Club gained spiritual tattoos from special relics in their possession.

  • The Fool: four black dots which happened to form a small square.
  • Justice: a star.
  • The Hanged Man: a hexagonal crystal-like snowflake.
  • The Sun: a circle with lines extending out from the edge.

In situations in which they could not pray, they could use the triggering of the corresponding tattoo to skip the chanting of the honorific name. There is no way to transmit any information using this method, but it could attract Mr. Fool's attention, allowing him to cast "His" gaze over in critical situations.

When Madam Magician asked Lumian Lee to draw his Major Arcana card, he initially drew The World card. But, as the position had been filled, Madam Magician asked him to draw again, after which Lumian drew two cards. On his left, representing the male card, was The Chariot. On his right, representing the female card, was The Empress. In the end, Lumian chose the left card.
