Son of Chaos

The Anarchy · Genie of the Magic Wishing Lamp

Overview

The Son of Chaos is the third child of the Original Creator, and is one of his three direct children along with the Mother Goddess of Depravity and the Mother Tree of Desire.1

He was the original owner of the Nation of Disorder and its corresponding Pathways, the Black Emperor and Justiciar. He was also the original owner of the Chaos Mist Pathway.

Then came the Son of Chaos, who established order, and with it, shadows naturally emerged.

The Second Day of Genesis, Book 2, chapter 1171.

Honorific Name

The Anarchy, Shadow of Order, Bridge of Light and Darkness, Interstice Between Black and White, Ever-Uncertain Mist, The Truth that Oversees All Things.2

This is the complete honorific name, formed from combining the honorific names of The Anarchy and the Uncertain Mist.

Symbols, Authorities and Abilities

In the original Chinese text, 律法 (lǜfǎ) and 规律 (guīlǜ) are distinct terms with different nuances, though both can be translated as "Laws". Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page uses the same English words for the same Chinese words throughout.

  • Laws (律法, lǜfǎ): codified, enforceable laws, rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance. Officially translated as "laws and decrees" and "set of laws" in some relevant chapters.3
  • Principles (规律, guīlǜ): inherent patterns or natural laws, the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world. In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time.

The symbolism of Laws also uses different Chinese characters, 定律 (dìnglǜ),4 which are also the same characters used for the "Law" in the "Laws of Beyonder Characteristics" or "Second Law".

  • Laws (定律, dìnglǜ): fixed laws or established principles, a stable, universally applicable rule that does not change.

The Chinese word for the symbolism of Power (权力, Quánlì) does not denote the "raw power" sense, but rather the "power in the social and political" sense.

Pathway authorities

The complete Son of Chaos possesses full authority over the Justiciar, Black Emperor, and Chaos Mist Pathways and the corresponding symbols, which include but are not limited to the following:

  • Justiciar: authorities over Order, Judgement, Balance, and Disorder.
  • Black Emperor: authorities over Autocrat and Disorder.
  • Chaos Mist: authorities over Deconstruction, Truth, and Chaos.

Symbols at the Above the Sequence level

  • Chaos (混沌, Hùndùn): as one of the three direct children of the Original Creator, the Son of Chaos inherently possesses the Chaos quality of the Original Creator.5
    • The Chaos Mist is the manifestation of Chaos in the Son of Chaos.6
    • As the Primordial Hunger possesses Primordium and Chaos qualities, he prioritized devouring the Sefirot and Great Old Ones related to those qualities the most. Among the Outer Deities, he preferred the Uncertain Mist the most,7 because he is the Chaos Gastric Juices, while the Uncertain Mist is a part of the Son of Chaos.8
  • Order (秩序, Zhìxù) and Shadow of Order (秩序阴影, Zhìxù yīnyǐng): the birth of the Son of Chaos established Order in the universe, and with it, the Shadow of Order naturally emerged.9
    • Order: the symbolism established with the birth of the Son of Chaos. He can modify the bottom-layered Rules of the world to some extent, establish new non-bottom-layered Rules that stay permanent, as well as remove non-bottom-layered Rules.10
      • The Justiciar possesses the corresponding core authority of Order, with subordinate authorities of Rules (规则, Guīzé), Laws (律法, Lǜ fǎ), Principles (规律, Guīlǜ), and others.
      • Through the definition of Rules, and only at the level of the complete Son of Chaos, can he touch the Astral World, defining concepts and influencing symbols.11
      • The Lord of Mysteries, Demon of Knowledge, and Son of Chaos joining force can extend the Chaos Primogenitor's cooldown by modifying the Major Month. However, they could extend it by one Major Year at most.12
      • The Son of Chaos can order all dimensions (higher, lower, and the current one) to become interconnected, as though overlaid with layer upon layer of glass barriers, as shown by the Celestial Thearch in The Apocalypse.13
        • If this rule was established, the High-Dimensional Overseer could no longer increase dimensions unscathed, and the Son of Chaos could still maintain influence on the battlefield even if his dimension is lowered.
      • The death due to the backlash from Above the Sequence Rules, such as by exceeding three wishes of the Magic Wishing Lamp, will be granted even when one was isolated by the Corpse Cathedral of the Visionary.14
      • After making a deal with Klein, the Genie's voice gradually turned solemn, as if he was proclaiming a clause of law (法律, Fǎlǜ). He seemed to have established a particular Rule based on this deal. There was no need to worry that Klein would go back on his word in the future, even if he were to become a Lord of Mysteries.
        • This is a Rule that has been set. The Genie himself can't violate it afterwards.
      • Laws (定律, Dìnglǜ): the symbolism of Laws, held by both the Son of Chaos and the Demon of Knowledge. One focuses on the law and the universe, the other on the knowledge of the law itself.15
        • The complete Son of Chaos can even influence the Laws of Beyonder Characteristics such as Indestructibility, Convergence, and Separation to a certain extent, making limited, low-level, and small-scale modifications.16
      • Power (权力, Quánlì): Order and Power are symbiotic, Order requires Power, and Power requires Order, thus the Son of Chaos encompasses the symbolism of Power.17
    • Shadow of Order: the symbolism that naturally emerged from the Order established after the birth of the Son of Chaos.
      • At the core of the Black Emperor is the authority of Autocrat (独裁者, Dúcáizhě) under the Shadow of Order symbolism, and the majority of the Disorder (混乱, Hùnluàn) authority.18
      • Distortion (扭曲, Niǔqū): a subordinate authority of Autocrat.
        • The Son of Chaos can unleash his powers upon the outside world and Distort all surrounding concepts, authorities, and symbolisms, as shown by the Celestial Thearch in The Apocalypse.19
        • The Son of Chaos can Distort the sentence from "I can promise" to "I promise", making it impossible for someone to go back on their word. If they did, they would suffer damage at the level of a Great Old One.20 This can be done even after he has become the Genie, losing his Sefirah, Uniqueness, and Beyonder Characteristics, reduced to mere consciousness, spirit, and status.21
          • If the true Celestial Worthy made a promise to send the Magic Wishing Lamp into the Cosmos in the future, the Genie can Distort this promise to make his seal be removed automatically.
            • This effect can only be achieved by twisting the original owner's words, not the words of someone else. This is one of the more than ten ways for the Genie to verify that someone is the true Celestial Worthy or not.
          • The Genie could Distort the Dual-Pathway True God Amon's sentence of "I can also promise to send you back to the cosmos" and make it impossible for him to go back on his word.
        • The Genie could argue with or twist the Dual-Pathway King of Angels Lumian's ideas. However, since he was merely the messenger for The Fool, not the decision-maker, this wouldn't affect the outcome.
      • Resurrection: Death is a part of the most underlying and core part of the Order of Reality, thus the Black Emperor can Resurrect.
        • In the First Epoch, by killing himself and then Resurrecting through the Black Emperor ability, the Son of Chaos was able to send his main consciousness past the Astral Barrier when it was still perfect.22
    • Gray (灰色, Huīsè): the Chaos Mist of the Uncertain Mist is the Gray between white and black, the fog between light and darkness, the bridge between Order and Shadows of Order of the Son of Chaos.23
  • Contracts and Transactions: he can make Contracts and facilitate Transactions that even Great Old Ones have to honor.
    • The Genie can make Contracts that if not honored will cause even a Lord of Mysteries to perish and split.
      • Although both the Son of Chaos and the Lord of Mysteries have means to resist the backlash and reduce the damage caused by breaching the Contract made with their own Sefirot as witnesses, it won't be without a price. They would need to consider the consequences.24
    • Without the Uncertain Mist present to coordinate and unify the Outer Deities into an alliance, none dared bet on receiving support if they stayed behind to deal with the Lord of Mysteries.25
    • Without the Uncertain Mist, the Outer Deities would be disunited, each fighting for themselves, and would easily be driven back by Earth's Above the Sequences after The Apocalypse.26
  • Overseeing: with the strengthened Overseeing ability at the Great Old One level, the Uncertain Mist, and by extension the Son of Chaos, can only be harmed by existences possessing a Sefirah.27

Appearance

As the Genie: the wick at the spout of the Magic Wishing Lamp suddenly ignited, emitting light that looked like viscous water, spurting upwards to form a blurred and distorted pale golden figure.28

History

Genesis

On the first day, the Original Creator awoke and created this world, but Reality, the Spirit World, the Astral World, and Fate were still mixed together.

On the second day, the Oldest One birthed the Mother Goddess of Depravity. Reality gained a foundation, the Spirit World and Astral World were separated, and the River of Fate began to flow. Soon after, the Mother Tree of Desire was born, giving rise to life and introducing evil and desire. Then came the Son of Chaos, who established order, and with it, shadows naturally emerged.29

First Epoch

As one of the direct children of the Original Creator, the Son of Chaos was the most affected by the Law of Convergence when the Original Creator awakened in the Pre-Epoch, causing part of his Sefirah (later self-completed into the Nation of Disorder) and the two Pathways of Justiciar and Black Emperor to be separated from him and be attracted to Earth.

The Son of Chaos was able to send his main consciousness past the Astral Barrier by killing himself and then resurrecting through the Black Emperor ability.30 However, he encountered the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings and was controlled by him.31 What remained of him in the Cosmos became known as the Outer Deity Uncertain Mist, with some consciousness.32

At the start of the Era of Destruction, the Son of Chaos was sealed inside the Magic Wishing Lamp by the Celestial Worthy, and became the Genie.33

Fourth Epoch

In the Fourth Epoch, Trunsoest attempted to shatter the Magic Wishing Lamp, but was unsuccessful. This led to his corruption.34

Lord of Mysteries

The Magic Wishing Lamp was formerly owned by Roselle Gustav. Later, it was held by Bernadette Gustav and she made a wish for it in preparation for the upcoming fight against William Augustus I during George III's Apotheosis Ritual. Through its power, she was able to hold the power of a Sequence 1 Knowledge Emperor for a day.35

The artifact was temporarily held by Cattleya when Bernadette left to find the remains of Roselle on a mysterious island.36 Afterwards, she informed Mr. Fool about the lamp. When Klein looked at the Magic Wishing Lamp through Cattleya's star in Sefirah Castle, the Genie took over her star and entered in her place. In their meeting, he confirmed that Klein is not the previous owner of Sefirah Castle that sealed him. He still made his offer to Klein, his release in exchange for his assistance, confident that Klein will need it in the future.37

When Klein was trapped in Adam's Corpse Cathedral, he made a deal with the Genie in order to escape. He promised to send the Magic Wishing Lamp into the Cosmos before the end of 1368, in exchange for two wishes. His first wish was to move the two wishes used by Bernadette under his name, so that she wouldn't die by using all three wishes. His second wish was for Utopia to give rise to a corresponding region in the spirit world, completing his Sequence 1, Attendant of Mysteries ritual. As his second wish is equivalent to his fourth wish, it was distorted, killing him and helping him escape Adam's Divine Kingdom.

Klein rented the lamp from Bernadette before he went to the Hornacis Mountain Range. He made a deal with the Genie to acquire assistance during his apotheosis ritual. During the ritual, the Genie used his connection with the Trunsoest Brass Book to use it to help Klein against the assault of Amon's avatars.38 When the Celestial Worthy was about to be revived in Klein's original body, the Genie used the Trunsoest Brass Book to prohibit his resurrection.39

During The Fool's Gambit, when Amon invaded Sefirah Castle, the Genie continued using the Trunsoest Brass Book in Klein's fight against Amon. However, when Amon also promised the Genie to send him back to the Cosmos, Klein didn't want to take a chance and used the Fool's authority, forcing the Genie to shrink back to the Magic Wishing Lamp.40

Circle of Inevitability

The lamp appeared with Bernadette around the period before Vermonda Sauron's death at Medici's hands. The Genie suggested to save Roselle by balancing his corruption with another's.41

In the Tarot Club's battle against Loki, The Hermit held the lamp.42

During Project Vortex in 1359 of the Fifth Epoch, the Genie was used as a medium to gain temporary access to the Nation of Disorder. Both the Genie inside the Magic Wishing Lamp and his counterpart in the Cosmos, the Uncertain Mist, were heavily injured by the combined efforts of Adam, Amon, Roselle Gustav and Bernadette Gustav.43

Before the Death of Primordial Demoness, Lumian Lee visited Bernadette and asked to speak with the Genie for advice regarding The Apocalypse on behalf of The Fool. The Genie gave three hints that greatly assisted them in The Apocalypse.44

In the early stage of The Apocalypse, Mirror Roselle Gustav mentioned to Bernadette that even with the required Uniquenesses and Sequence 1 characteristic, the Nation of Disorder couldn't immediately become a Great Old One because its consciousness is not unified. The only way to instantly elevate it to the level of a Great Old One was to unseal and release the Genie; however, what he might do after escaping his seal was entirely unpredictable.45

After The Apocalypse, Klein intended to fulfill his promise to return the Magic Wishing Lamp to the universe, using it to lure out the hiding Uncertain Mist and secretly influence him. Without the Uncertain Mist, the Outer Deities would be disunited, and would easily be driven away.46

The Genie and the Uncertain Mist are hostile towards each other.47 Whether the Uncertain Mist or the Genie would emerge victorious after they merged is unknown, both scenarios are equally plausible.48
