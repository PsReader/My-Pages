Patriarch Pathway

Outer Deity: Mother Tree of Desire · Boon advancement

The Patriarch pathway, also called the Actor or Desires pathway, combines desire with blathering, madness, and propagation. It satisfies the Beyonder’s own desires while imposing twisted desires on others through acting, dreams, influence, and life-draining techniques. Advancement on Earth comes through Boons granted by the Mother Tree of Desire.

Paraphrased from the Lord of the Mysteries Wiki pathway article.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Manipulates desires in the hearts of creatures and can induce twisted impulses in other people.
  • Awakens survival instincts across a region, even affecting powerful beings and destabilizing their judgment.
  • Spreads corruption through dreams, acting, environmental influence, and the mutation of living bodies.
  • Its Outer Deity corrupted the Chained God and the Dark Side of the Universe, turning desire into an engine of indiscriminate violence, sacrifice, and biological transformation.

Corruption from Mother Tree of Desire

The Mother Tree of Desire is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Known corruptive effects

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Mother Tree of Desire. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
