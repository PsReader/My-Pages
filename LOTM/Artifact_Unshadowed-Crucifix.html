Unshadowed Crucifix

Grade 1 · No code recorded

Overview

Unshadowed Crucifix is recorded in the archive as a Grade 1 Sealed Artifact. Grade 1 artifacts sit near Saint level, roughly matching Sequence 3 to Sequence 4 Beyonders.

It is described as: A bronze thorned cross.

Function

By sacrificing some blood to it, it will exude a bright light that purifies any evil influences, it will purify beyonder characteristics too. In addition to its purifying effects, this item also has Demigod abilities in the field of 'Sun', such as "Sun Flare."

Downside

Cannot be worn with other Beyonder item, slowly degrading user sequence if user is not an angel, will turn the user into a pious Sun believer.

Status

Destroyed

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
