Fifth Epoch

The Iron Age—an era of industry, empires, colonialism, war, and the slow creep of an approaching apocalypse.

The Fifth Epoch, more commonly known as the Iron Age, began roughly 1349 years before the start of the novel. With the gods weakened after the Conflicts of the Seven Gods, humanity slowly rebuilds from the ruins of the Fourth Epoch, ushering in a new age of steam, gunpowder, and rapid change.

Major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries and Circle of Inevitability follow.

Overview

After the devastation of the Fourth Epoch, the world enters a long period of rebuilding. Gods no longer openly descend, and while the Seven Orthodox Churches remain powerful, their influence is tempered by rising nations and the slow march of secular change. In the shadows, secret organizations, cults, and hidden orders continue to pursue the path of Beyonders.

What defines the Iron Age most is progress. Through Roselle Gustav's inventions and the spread of steam power, the Northern Continent undergoes an Industrial Revolution that reshapes society. Nations expand overseas, carving up the Southern Continent and the Sonia Sea for resources and territory. However, this prosperity is built on blood, exploitation, and the ever-growing tension between the Great Powers.

As the barrier between reality and the cosmos weakens, the gaze of the Outer Deities turns back to the world. Boon Recipients multiply, ancient seals fray, and by the time of the novel, the Fifth Epoch finds itself on the brink of another cataclysm.

Early Fifth Epoch (0–359 AE) Years 0–359

The Fifth Epoch begins more than 1300 years before the events of the story. In the Northern Continent, the Seven Orthodox Churches and the Four Great Nations establish a new order. While public life is dominated by the churches and nation-states, a vast network of secret organizations operates in the shadows, many of them devoted to the mystic arts.

In the Southern Continent, the fall of Death leads to upheaval. The Resistance emerges, and kingdoms such as the Highlands Kingdom and the Paz Kingdom are founded in the power vacuum left behind.

New esoteric groups also take root during this period, including the Theosophy Order, the Life School of Thought, and the Blood Sanctify Sect.

Twenty-Year War and White Rose War 359–624 AE

Conflict between the Great Powers dominates the middle centuries. The Loen Kingdom and the Feysac Empire clash in the Twenty-Year War (359–624 AE), ending in Loen's defeat and the loss of Sonia Island.

Not long after, the White Rose War erupts between Loen and Intis. This time, Loen emerges victorious and regains its former prestige. During this period of rising nationalism and religious tension, the Church of the Earth Mother and the Church of Wisdom grow increasingly divided, their split hastened by the machinations of other Orthodox Gods.

Battle of the Violated Oath 738 AE

Longstanding friction between the Church of the Earth Mother and the Church of Wisdom in Feynapotter erupts into open conflict. Loen and Intis intervene under the pretense of protecting religious freedom, drawing Feysac into the war in an attempt to contain the situation.

The war lasts five years and is remembered as the Battle of the Violated Oath, as both sides accuse the other of breaking their solemn vows from the end of the Fourth Epoch. In its aftermath, Lenburg, Masin, and Segar gain independence along Loen and Intis' borders with Feynapotter and become strongholds of the Church of Wisdom. The Church of the Earth Mother is left as the sole faith of the Feynapotter Kingdom.

870–1055 AE A Period of Relative Peace

Central authority strengthens across the Northern Continent. Large-scale wars subside in favor of smaller, localized conflicts. Gunpowder becomes widespread, bringing profound changes to warfare. Peace and technological advancement lead many to believe the supernatural is mere myth or legend, and pre-Fifth Epoch history is relegated to fairy tales in the eyes of the public.

Roselle and the Industrial Revolution 1173–1198 AE

"There are some things that make it easier to be ‘infected’ the more you know."

The Fifth Epoch's greatest turning point comes with the transmigration of Huang Tao into the body of Roselle Gustav. Blessed with the memories of his previous life and the power of the Savant pathway, Roselle drives the continent into the Industrial Revolution.

He invents the steam engine, improves sailing vessels, and creates countless other innovations born from his modern knowledge. The new steamships and improved trade routes give rise to a golden age of commerce and piracy. The God of Craftsmanship rebrands himself as the God of Steam and Machinery to align with this new era of progress.

In 1173, Roselle overthrows the Intis Kingdom and becomes consul of the Intis Republic. Through a series of victories, he transforms the Republic into the Intis Empire and crowns himself Caesar the Great in 1192. That same year, he drafts the Civil Code, invents Tarot cards and poker, and creates the Cards of Blasphemy containing the twenty-two Pathways.

Roselle also dispatches expeditions in search of the Forsaken Land of the Gods and the Western Continent, and in 1194, he discovers a safe sea route to the Southern Continent—igniting the age of colonization. In 1198, he is assassinated in the White Maple Palace by the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun alongside much of the former Sauron royal family. Though he had ascended to Sequence 0 Black Emperor with the aid of the Twilight Hermit Order, his resurrection fails, and the Uniqueness and Characteristics are reclaimed.

In the decades that follow, organizations such as the Element Dawn, the Iron and Blood Cross Order, and the Aurora Order rise to prominence.

Colonization of the Southern Continent Post-1194 AE

With the safe route established, the Great Powers of the North descend upon the Southern Continent. The already-fractured Balam Empire collapses as the Highlands Kingdom and Paz Kingdom fall to northern invasion.

The continent is carved up between Loen and Intis, with Feysac also staking its claim. East Balam falls largely under Loen's control, while West Balam becomes a bloody battleground between Loen and Intis. The Kings of Loen adopt the title of Emperor of the Balam Empire to legitimize their rule.

Plantations and manors spread across the land, and countless locals are enslaved or forced into servitude. The Orthodox Churches attempt to convert the population away from the faith of Death, but they find stiff resistance. The Numinous Episcopate, controlled by the Eggers Family, remains active, and faith in Death persists amongst the lower classes. The Psychology Alchemists are also founded around this time.

Colonization of the Sonia Sea Pre- and Post-Roselle

Even before Roselle's voyages, Northern nations had begun exploring the Sonia Sea. Loen had occupied Bansy Harbor roughly three hundred years prior and Damir Harbor over two hundred years ago.

Within the last fifty years before the modern era, the Rorsted Archipelago is fully colonized by Loen. Formerly devoted to the Sea God Kalvetua, the islands are brought into the Loen fold. The Gargas Archipelago falls to the Feysac Empire.

World War c. 1349 AE (Novel Context)

Tensions finally explode into a full-scale world war engulfing the Northern Continent. Old rivalries, colonial grudges, and the ambitions of great powers all converge into a conflict of unprecedented scale.

The war is fought not only with rifles and cannons, but also with Beyonders. High-Sequence combatants turn the tide of battles, secret organizations sabotage their enemies from the shadows, and the influence of hidden cults grows with the chaos of war.

By the time of the novel's opening, the war is still raging, casting a dark pall over the continent and further weakening the already frayed barrier against greater threats.

Post-War 1349–1350 AE

The war continues to grind on, exacting a horrific toll on all sides. Nations are drained of men and resources, industry is repurposed for weapons of war, and the common people suffer in the trenches and slums alike.

Amidst this chaos, the veil between worlds grows thinner. More and more people are drawn into the world of mysticism, whether willingly or by force. The stage is set for the events of Lord of the Mysteries.

Lord of Mysteries Year 1349 AE

In the city of Tingen, in the Loen Kingdom, Zhou Mingrui awakens in the body of Klein Moretti. What begins as an attempt to survive in a strange world quickly spirals into a grand tapestry of conspiracy, divinity, and destiny.

Through the Tarot Club and his own relentless pursuit of knowledge and strength, Klein navigates the treacherous waters of the Beyonder world, slowly uncovering the truth of Sefirah Castle, the history of the epochs, and the looming threat that hangs over all of reality.

The Outer Deities' Invasion Approaching

As the Fifth Epoch draws towards its later years, the pressure from beyond reality intensifies. The seals holding back the Outer Deities grow ever weaker, and their influence seeps into the world through dreams, madness, and Boons.

Cults multiply, apocalyptic prophecies spread, and even the weakened Orthodox Gods find themselves struggling to hold back the tide. What was once distant whispers becomes an unavoidable reality—the Iron Age may well be the last age of humanity as it knows it.
