Volume 7: The Hanged Man

Quote

The Hanged Man is sacrifice and perspective., Cuttlefish That Loves Diving

Overview

The Hanged Man is the seventh volume of the Lord of Mysteries series.

Title Meaning

The Hanged Man represents sacrifice, perspective, and reversal of fate as characters confront their destinies.

Synopsis

The Final Trials

Klein faces unprecedented challenges as the stakes reach their highest point. The world trembles as forces far beyond mortal comprehension begin to awaken from their ancient slumber.

Secrets of the World

The mystery of the world deepens as ancient secrets long buried and forgotten powers resurface. Klein must confront truths that challenge his very existence and understanding of reality.

Reversal of Fate

Faced with seemingly impossible odds, Klein must overturn the destiny that has been written for him. His will and cunning are tested to their absolute limits as he fights against the very fabric of fate itself.
