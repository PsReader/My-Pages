Staff of the Stars

Grade 0 · Code 0-62

Overview

Staff of the Stars (0-62) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: A black cane with embedded gems.

Function

  • While holding the staff, using corresponding scenes in your mind, holder will directly descend to their desired destination. The prerequisite was that the outlined scene had to be absolutely correct.
  • While holding the Staff, if some Beyonder powers or figures appear in your mind, this cane will reenact the corresponding powers and person.
  • Any foes struck by it will randomly mutate or encounter various strange effects.

Downside

If the staff is left unsealed with no one holding it, various anomalies would appear around it unpredictably.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
