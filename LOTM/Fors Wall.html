Character profile

Fors Wall

Door · Sequence 4: Secrets Sorcerer (Door Pathway)

Fors Wall is a Beyonder of the Apprentice. Her codename in the Tarot Club is The Magician. She is a bestselling novelist, and good friends with Xio Derecha. Later, she became a disciple of Dorian Gray Abraham, a member of the Abraham Family.

This page includes major-spoiler information from the linked source article. The fact fields below are transcribed from that article’s character infobox.
