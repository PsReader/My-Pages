Misfortune Cloth Puppet

Grade 3 · Code 3-0625

Overview

Misfortune Cloth Puppet (3-0625) is recorded in the archive as a Grade 3 Sealed Artifact. Grade 3 artifacts are the lowest sealed class, roughly Sequence 8 to Sequence 9.

It is described as: A cloth puppet wearing a regal gown.

Function

It brings misfortune to people nearby, although they have to be near it for a sufficiently long period of time for it to work.

Downside

Affects the user as well.

Status

No status is recorded in the source table.

Source: the Sealed Artifacts archive, from the Lord of the Mysteries wiki.
