Character profile

Derrick Berg

Sun · Sequence 4: Unshadowed (Sun Pathway)

Derrick Berg is a Beyonder of the Bard Pathway. Codename The Sun in the Tarot Club. He was a teenager who lives in the City of Silver, which resides in the Forsaken Land of the Gods. He hopes he can lead the City of Silver out of the Forsaken Land of the Gods, restore sunlight, and bring normal lives back to his people.

Complete quote entries transcribed from the character’s dedicated wiki Quotes page. The collection includes available Book One and Book Two entries.

I don’t care if you’re God or The Fool, my prayers will not change. I hope that the people of the City of Silver will be freed from the curse of their destinies. I hope that the sun and sky described in the books will appear in our skies. If possible—if possible, I wish that my parents can be revived.

— Derrick Berg, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 138.

So, I want to be the Sun… When there was a Sun shining over the land, we had never encountered any curse.

— Derrick Berg, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 138.

Yes, we believe that we will regain the Lord’s favor in the end. Perhaps, it will be on the day the sun rises again. We were once ruled by the giants’ royal family, and we worshiped the Giant King Aurmir. Later, we were saved by the Lord and we will never betray the Lord again.

— Derrick answered to The Fool in an uncertain tone, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 180.

Did the Lord not abandon us? It’s just that “He” had gone mad.

— Derrick Berg, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 311.

My faith lies only with Mr. Fool!

— Derrick Berg, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 1172.

Mr. World, do you need to bring some food with you? There are mushrooms that can produce milk!

— Derrick to Mr. World, Lord of Mysteries, Chapter 1176.
