Cattleya

Hermit · Sequence 3: Clairvoyant (Hermit Pathway)

Cattleya is one of the 6 Pirate Kings and is a Beyonder of the Mystery Pryer. Her codename in the Tarot Club is The Hermit. She is one of the ten Pillars of the Moses Ascetic Order. She once held the title Admiral of Stars. Her flagship is named "The Future" and she is the captain of The Stars Pirates. Her bounty originally was 37,000 pounds, after she permitted Gehrman Sparrow board the Future, her bounty raised to 45,000 pounds. By year 1352, Cattleya became the hidden Pirate Queen. After Mr. Fool had entered a deep sleep, she finally made up her mind and announced her advancement to the public and became a true Pirate Queen called Queen of Stars.
