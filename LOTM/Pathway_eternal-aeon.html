Eternal Aeon Pathway

Outer Deity: Circle of Inevitability · Boon advancement

The Eternal Aeon pathway, also called the Dancer or Inevitability pathway, represents predetermined and decisive fate. It specializes in ritual dances, contracts, fate prediction, fate manipulation, and accumulating power from other beings. The Circle of Inevitability grants its Earth-side advancement through Boons rather than standard potions.

Non-standard pathway lore and Outer Deity corruption details contain major story spoilers.

High-level abilities

The pathway’s upper abilities are inseparable from the corruption of its Outer Deity. These powers are expressed through Boons, mystical influence, and the gradual replacement of ordinary cause-and-effect with the pathway’s domain.

  • Anchors a chosen tributary of the River of Fate so that interference with a designated project becomes increasingly doomed to fail.
  • Uses Cycle and Loop to reset regions, undo developments, and force events or battles to repeat.
  • Fixes a person’s fate onto madness or suffering, preserving the body while turning the future into a predetermined catastrophe.
  • Uses Substitution to replace figures in the River of Fate, switch bodies or identities, and create profound mystical links for pursuit.

Corruption from Circle of Inevitability

The Circle of Inevitability is the pathway’s Outer Deity and primary source of Boons. Corruption can appear as physical mutation, mental contamination, altered fate, environmental collapse, or the rewriting of reality, depending on the pathway’s authority.

Advancement warning

Unlike the standard pathways, Earth-side advancement is recorded as receiving a Boon through prayer to Circle of Inevitability. That connection is also the main route through which the Outer Deity’s will, symbols, and corruption can enter the practitioner.
