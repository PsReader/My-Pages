Third Epoch

The Glorious Era—an age of unity under God Almighty, the ascension of the Orthodox Gods, and the rise of the Solomon Empire before its slow descent into ruin.

The Third Epoch is known solely as the Glorious Era. Following humanity's revolt at the end of the Second Epoch, the figure known as Adam, who proclaimed himself God Almighty, unified the continent under his rule. It is during this era that the orthodox faiths are established, the Pathway system is codified into the churches, and the first great human empire reaches a height of prosperity and mysticism unseen since before the Dark Epoch.

Overview

Dubbed the "Glorious Era", the Third Epoch is remembered as the pinnacle of human civilization in recorded history. For the first time since the First Epoch, humanity was not enslaved to the Ancient Gods of the Second Epoch. Under the unifying rule of God Almighty, the Northern and Central Continents are brought under a singular order. Mysticism flourishes, the churches become the cornerstone of both faith and governance, and the pursuit of becoming a Beyonder is systematized and sanctified across the vast majority of the human population.

Despite its name, the Glorious Era is defined by a slow, insidious rot beneath its splendor. Zealotry, schisms, ambition amongst the nobility, increasing infighting between the Orthodox Churches, and the ever growing desire for godhood amongst those of high Sequence all eat away at the utopia that God Almighty had forced into existence. By the end of the epoch, that glory would collapse into total war.

God Almighty and the New Order

At the close of the Second Epoch's Dawn Era, the man known as Adam rose to prominence. He would come to be known as God Almighty, the Ancient Sun God returned to his full authority. Utilizing his immense power and authority over the Sun Pathway, he spearheaded humanity's final revolt, personally slaying the Elf King and Dragon of Imagination, gravely wounding the Giant King and Phoenix Ancestor, and forcing the Devil Monarch to sacrifice the vast majority of his strength to seal the Abyss.

With the rule of the Ancient Gods shattered, God Almighty united humanity under his banner. He positioned himself not as a king of man, but as the sole Creator-God of the current world, the Omniscient and Omnipotent God. His reign was absolute. The notion of divine right was no longer contested, for the one that broke the yoke of the Ancient Gods now sat above all creation. Under his rule, heretical faiths were purged, the Pathways were distributed and controlled through the upcoming Orthodox order, and a new golden age was forged.

God Almighty would rule for the vast majority of the Glorious Era. However, towards its latter half, his activities become more secretive. His gaze turns inwards and towards greater existences, his presence within the material world grows more distant, and his direct intervention becomes exceedingly rare. Many attribute this to his attempts to accommodate more power, to reclaim what was lost, or to ascend even further beyond his current state. Whatever the truth may be, his growing absence would prove catastrophic for the order he created.

The Seven Orthodox Gods

In order to solidify his rule and to create a stable order that could persist, God Almighty elevated a select few to godhood. These beings would come to be known as the Seven Orthodox Gods. Each would be entrusted with their own authority, their own Pathway, and their own flock, acting as the pillars of the new divine order whilst still ultimately falling under the dominion of God Almighty.

Each of these deities holds dominion over a cluster of related Pathways, granting them immense influence over their respective Churches. Whilst their faiths coexisted under the banner of God Almighty for the majority of the era, doctrine, territory, and the scramble for influence would breed tension with each passing century. Many suspect that not all seven ascended through the same means, and that the interests of each god had already begun to diverge long before the era's end.

Establishment of the Orthodox Churches

With the Seven Orthodox Gods ascended, God Almighty commissioned the establishment of the Orthodox Churches. These were not mere places of worship, but colossal, state-encompassing organizations. They acted as the arbiters of faith, the distributors of Beyonder resources, the enforcers of doctrine, the educators of the masses, and in many regions, the direct extensions of imperial authority.

The Churches were built with extreme care around the Pathway system. Through sanctioned ritual, prayer, and the distribution of potions, they controlled the spread of mysticism throughout humanity. Becoming a Beyonder was no longer a matter of stumbling into chaos as it was in the Dark Epoch, but could be done through the Church's sanctioned paths, albeit only under their strict supervision and usually only for those deemed loyal or useful.

The most prominent of these would come to dominate the continent. The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun, the Church of the Lord of Storms, the Church of Evernight, the Church of Steam and Machinery, the Church of Knowledge and Wisdom, the Church of the Earth Mother, and the Church of the God of Combat all established cathedrals, seminaries, inquisitorial orders, and their own internal hierarchies of Beyonders. Often, a single diocese would see fierce competition between the orders of differing Churches.

God Almighty's own faith remained supreme, preached as the worship of the Omniscient and Omnipotent God above all. In practice, however, the vast majority of the populace would find themselves worshipping one of the Seven Orthodox Gods as their patron far more often than they would directly worship God Almighty himself.

The Solomon Empire

As the Glorious Era progressed, temporal power coalesced into what would become the greatest human empire of the age: the Solomon Empire. Blessed by God Almighty and supported by nearly every Orthodox Church, the Solomon Empire unified the Northern Continent under its banner, subjugating the last holdouts of old kingdoms and bringing them into the new divine order.

The Solomon Empire was the embodiment of the Glorious Era. Its cities were grand beyond compare, its cathedrals pierced the clouds, its academies pushed the boundaries of both orthodox theology and mysticism, and its armies were bolstered by countless sanctioned Beyonders from across the Churches. Trade flourished, the Hermes Language spread to every corner of the empire, and for the first time in history, humanity could rightfully call itself the ruler of the known world rather than its slave.

The imperial family, staunch adherents to the faith of God Almighty, were believed to be divinely ordained to rule in his name during his absence. Emperors would oftentimes be anointed by the highest representatives of the Churches, with the implicit understanding that the throne existed to uphold God's will and to act as the sword of the divine order.

For centuries, the Solomon Empire reigned as an uncontested colossus. However, as the centuries wore on, imperial authority began to wane. The Churches grew too powerful for even the Emperor to rein in, the nobility grew decadent and power hungry, and the belief in God Almighty's imminent return began to be replaced with politicking, ambition, and heretical thoughts of godhood. Whispers of the empire's "glory" began to sour into obsession, envy, and greed.

Sephirot, Pathways and the Divine Order

The Glorious Era is also notable for the immense progress made in understanding Beyonder pathways. Building off of the groundwork laid at the very end of the Second Epoch, the Churches, under the supervision of God Almighty and the Orthodox Gods, began to fully compile, verify and sanctify potion formulas for the vast majority of known Pathways.

It was during this era that the concept of the Sephirot became more widely known amongst the upper echelons of the Churches. The twenty-two pathways were categorized and tied to the divine order, with each Orthodox God having their own domain over a set of them. The idea of climbing the Sequence ladder in service to a god became the "correct" and "holy" way of advancement, as opposed to the reckless, blasphemous and chaotic pursuit of godhood that had plagued the previous epochs.

God Almighty himself was understood to stand above all Pathways. The Seven Orthodox Gods acted as his deputies, each a lord over their own portion of authority. The Churches would go to great lengths to demonize any form of Beyonder advancement that did not fall under their sanctioned routes, branding them as heresy, the work of evil gods, or a return to the chaos of the Dark Epoch.

Despite this control, the thirst for power proved impossible to fully suppress. Countless secret organizations, cults worshipping the Outer Deities, hidden families attempting to usurp authority, and ambitious individuals seeking to become gods in their own right all festered beneath the gilded surface of the Glorious Era.

Decline and the Road to the Fourth Epoch

The decline of the Third Epoch was not brought about by a single event, but by a culmination of centuries of rot. God Almighty's increasing absence created a power vacuum at the very top of the divine order. With their master growing distant, the Seven Orthodox Gods began to act more and more in their own interests, forming alliances, betraying one another, and competing for influence over the Solomon Empire and its vast territories.

The Churches, now behemoths with their own armies, lands, and legions of high-Sequence Beyonders, began to care less about serving God Almighty and more about expanding their own power. The Empire, caught in the middle, attempted to mediate and control, only to find itself increasingly powerless against the very institutions that were propping it up.

Tensions finally snapped into open conflict. The peace of the Glorious Era gave way to all out war between the Orthodox Churches, their vassals, and ambitious forces within the Empire. This conflict would come to be known as the precursor to the Warring Era of the Fourth Epoch. The Solomon Empire would be torn asunder, God Almighty's divine order would be shattered beyond repair, and the continent would be plunged into a new age of endless strife.

With the outbreak of war, the Glorious Era drew to a close. The age of unity was dead. The age of war had begun.
