Demoness Pathway

Sequence 9: Assassin → Sequence 0: Demoness

The Demoness Pathway centers on disaster and catastrophe, combining assassination, seduction, ritual and curse magic, invisible-thread manipulation, instigation, disease, and mirror-related abilities. At higher Sequences it expands into petrification, plagues, resurrection, natural disasters, apocalypse, and the return of things to chaos; its Above the Sequence is Calamity of Destruction and its Sefirah is City of Calamity.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its recurring themes are catastrophe, apocalypse, chaos, femininity, charm, Mirror World control, and dark magic. Signature tools include concealment, instigation, black flames, frost, curses, threads, disease/plague, petrification, mirror substitution and projection, with higher authorities governing calamities and final collapse.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Demoness of Despair: Plagues spread broadly, threads petrify on contact, charm overwhelms, and mirror projection and traversal intensify.
  • Sequence 3 — Demoness of Unaging: Mirror Self enables agelessness and resurrection through mirror persons; petrification, charm, curses, traversal, plagues, and projections strengthen.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Demoness of Catastrophe: Catastrophe governs natural, social, mechanical, and conceptual calamities, including mirror-world collapse and curses of destiny.
  • Sequence 1 — Demoness of Apocalypse: Mirror World manipulation and Apocalypse bring area-wide termination; disease authority expands beyond mystical pathogens, with archangel-grade dark magic.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Demoness: Chaos returns things toward primordial dissolution, joined by Primordium, conceptual charm, mirror-world rule, femininity, dark magic, and disease.

Novel lore & story appearances

Major spoilers for Lord of the Mysteries and Circle of Inevitability. The Demoness Pathway is framed around catastrophe and apocalypse, and the novel’s later exposition also associates it with the feminine side of the Original Creator. In the Fourth Epoch, its worshippers were organized as the Demoness Family; the group later widened recruitment into the Demoness Sect, devoted to the Primordial Demoness. During Backlund’s Great Smog, Ince Zangwill collaborated with the Sect and the royal faction, manipulating Trissy as a target. In the later plot against King George III’s apotheosis ritual, Trissy became a vessel for the Primordial Demoness and died when the goddess descended through her Sequence 4 body

Story connections

  • The Fourth-Epoch Demoness Family began as bloodline descendants of the Primordial Demoness; from the Fifth Epoch, it recruited non-relatives and became the Demoness Sect
  • In the Great Smog of Backlund, Ince Zangwill worked with the Demoness Sect and Loen’s royal family, using Trissy as a target in a plot that nearly killed Sherlock Moriarty
  • Trissy was treated as a possible vessel for the Primordial Demoness. During the struggle to stop King George III’s Black Emperor apotheosis, the goddess descended through her; Trissy died under the strain of hosting a god in a Sequence 4 body
  • In the Fourth Epoch’s War of the Four Emperors, the Primordial Demoness delivered the critical blow that stopped the Blood Emperor’s final attempt to drag the world into destruction, intending to resurrect him later

Figures to know

  • Primordial Demoness (Cheek)Sequence 0 deity worshipped by the Demoness Sect; central to the pathway’s Fourth-Epoch conflict and Trissy’s vessel plot
  • TrissyDemoness whose part in the Great Smog and the George III plot connects the Sect’s schemes to Klein’s story
  • Ince ZangwillFormer Church of Evernight member who allied with the Demoness Sect and the royal faction during the Great Smog
