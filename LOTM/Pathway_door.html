Door Pathway

Sequence 9: Apprentice → Sequence 0: Door

The Door Pathway centers on freedom of movement: phasing through barriers, travel through the Spirit World, teleportation, and using recorded powers. At higher Sequences, it expands into advanced space manipulation, concealment, sealing, precise locating, gravity effects, and alternate-space creation.

Pathway progression and late-sequence details contain major story spoilers.

Pathway themes & abilities

Its stated concepts are Door, Alternate Worlds, Seals, and Space. Core abilities include Door Opening, Astrology, Ability Recording, Teleportation, Space Manipulation, Concealment, Replication, Sealing, and Locating; the pathway is the main one associated with alternate spaces and illusory worlds, though the article leaves comparison with Sublunary Eye uncertain.

Sequence-specific highlights are shown in the progression table above.

High-sequence abilities

The upper Sequences change the pathway from a specialist discipline into a domain of angelic and divine authority. The highlights below summarize the progression from Sequence 4 upward; details contain major spoilers.

Sequences 4–3

  • Sequence 4 — Secrets Sorcerer: Conceals spaces behind doors, layers illusory doors defensively, hides secrets, exiles targets, and records angel-level powers.
  • Sequence 3 — Wanderer: Wanders through the Astral World and Cosmos, tears space, integrates with environments, and forms spatial cages.

Sequences 2–1

  • Sequence 2 — Planeswalker: Symbolizes self, views distant dimensions, stores scenes as toys, teleports directly, and opens doors to other realms.
  • Sequence 1 — Key of Stars: Manipulates stellar gravity to fragment space, locates targets precisely, and seals through mazes and starlight.

Sequence 0

  • Sequence 0 — Door: Commands doors, space, alternate worlds, and seals, enabling world-scale travel, sealing, alternate-world creation, and space destruction.

Novel lore & story appearances

The Door Pathway’s story significance centers on Bethel Abraham (“Mr. Door”), ancestor of the Abraham Family and a powerful Fourth Epoch angel. During the War of the Four Emperors, corruption linked to an Outer Deity led the Evernight Goddess and Lord of Storms to banish him to an isolated space of storms and darkness. His cries reached his descendants and drove many toward madness, obstructing the family’s advancement. Fors Wall and Dorian Gray expose the curse, while Verdu Garcia is manipulated by Amon and Medici into freeing Bethel. Bethel then gives his life to stop the Mother Goddess of Depravity’s descent, allowing Amon’s apotheosis; the family curse ends

Story connections

  • A prestigious family brought low: The Abrahams were major nobles under the Solomon and Tudor regimes, but Bethel’s banishment and maddening calls crippled their Beyonders and contributed to the family’s decline. Dorian Gray later learns that changing allegiance to Mr. Fool can temporarily lift the curse’s effects
  • Fors and Dorian trace the curse: Fors contacts Mr. Door and learns release conditions; Dorian explains the family’s blood curse. Verdu Garcia, seeking to free Bethel, is instead maneuvered by Amon and Medici into performing the release ritual
  • A release becomes an apotheosis crisis: Bethel returns while corrupted and begins advancing; the Mother Goddess of Depravity threatens to enter Earth through him. Bethel accepts self-sacrifice to stop that descent, completing Amon’s route to godhood; the Abraham curse is subsequently removed

Figures to know

  • Bethel Abraham / Mr. DoorDoor-pathway King of Angels and ancestor of the Abraham Family; his banishment is the source of the family’s curse
  • Fors WallDoor-pathway Beyonder who contacts Bethel and conveys the curse’s truth and release solution
