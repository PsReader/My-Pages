Die of Probability

Grade 0 · No code recorded

Overview

Die of Probability is recorded in the archive as a Grade 0 Sealed Artifact. Grade 0 artifacts are the most dangerous and most confidential class. Their power is comparable to an Angel, and their details are not to be inquired about or described.

It is described as: An ordinary die with red pips on each of its faces.

Function

Uncontrollably affects the luck of the user.

Downside

Uncontrollable.

Status

Accommodated
