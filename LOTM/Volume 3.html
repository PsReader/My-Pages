Volume 3: Traveler

Quote

"Every journey has its destination." — Cuttlefish That Loves Diving

Overview

Traveler is the third volume. Klein leaves Backlund and travels at sea under the identity of Gehrman Sparrow, meeting new friends and hunting pirates.

Title Meaning

The title refers to Klein's travels across the seas and his encounters with various people and creatures while maintaining his distance as a traveler.

Synopsis

The Wandering Detective

Klein sets sail across the Sonia Sea as Gehrman Sparrow, a wandering sailor and adventurer. This new identity allows him to explore distant lands while maintaining distance from his previous entanglements.

Encounters with Pirates and Mysteries

During his travels, Klein becomes entangled with pirates, pirate hunters, and various mysterious forces seeking the legendary Cards of Blasphemy. His journeys expose him to dangerous powers and forbidden knowledge that test both his wit and his growing abilities.

Spiritual Growth and Promotion

Through his maritime adventures and encounters with powerful adversaries, Klein gains valuable experience and spiritual insights. His journey leads to significant promotions along the Fool Pathway and an expanded sphere of influence across the seas.
