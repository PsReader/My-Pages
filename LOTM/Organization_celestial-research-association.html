Background

The Celestial Research Association is listed among the secret organizations associated with Monarch of Decay.

The affiliation record states that the Outer Deities who could not establish a single world-spanning faction instead seeded organizations of varying sizes in different regions. It does not give leadership, headquarters, membership, or history for this group, so none is assigned here.

Source note: Affiliation from the Outer Deities record and the secret organizations list. Some names come from author WeChat posts and some English renderings are unofficial.
