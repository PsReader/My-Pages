Character profile

Alger Wilson

Tyrant / Hanged Man · Sequence 4: Cataclysmic Interrer (Tyrant Pathway)

Alger Wilson is a Beyonder of the Sailor Pathway. The Captain of the Blue Avenger that once belonged to the Church of the Lord of Storms. He is one of the first members of the Tarot Club, codenamed The Hanged Man.

This page includes major-spoiler information from the linked source article. The fact fields below are transcribed from that article’s character infobox.
